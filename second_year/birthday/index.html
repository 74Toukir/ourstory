Happy Birthday Babe ❤️

Aaj ka din mere liye sirf ek birthday nahi, balki us din ki khushi hai jab is duniya ko tum jaise khoobsurat dil wala insaan mila tha. Shayad main kabhi lafzon mein nahi bata paungi ki tum meri zindagi mein kitni ahmiyat rakhti ho. Tum woh ho jo meri har dua mein ho, meri har muskurahat ki wajah ho aur mere dil ke sabse kareeb ho.

Jab tum meri zindagi mein aayi, tab maine samjha ki sachcha pyaar kya hota hai. Tumne mujhe sirf pyaar nahi diya, balki sukoon, respect aur woh ehsaas diya ki koi mujhe poori duniya se zyada samajhta hai. Tumhare saath bitaya hua har pal meri zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat hissa hai.

Main chahta hoon ki tumhari zindagi mein kabhi koi gham na aaye, tumhara chehra hamesha isi tarah muskurata rahe aur tumhari aankhon mein hamesha khushiyon ki chamak bani rahe.

Jo bhi sapne tum dekhti ho, Allah unhe haqeeqat bana de. Jo bhi manzil tum chaho, woh tumhe mile. Aur jo bhi khushi tum deserve karti ho, woh tumhare kadmon mein ho.

Tum mere liye sirf ek insaan nahi ho, tum meri aadat ho, meri dua ho, meri duniya ho. Agar mujhe zindagi dobara jeene ka mauka mile, to main har baar tumhe hi chununga. Kyunki tumhare jaisa koi nahi hai aur na hi kabhi ho sakta hai.

Aaj tumhare birthday par main sirf itna kehna chahta hoon ki chahe kitna bhi waqt guzar jaye, meri mohabbat tumhare liye kabhi kam nahi hogi. Har din ke saath yeh aur gehri, aur khoobsurat hoti jayegi. Main hamesha tumhare saath rehna chahta hoon, tumhari har khushi ka hissa banna chahti hoon aur tumhare har sapne ko poora hote dekhna chahta hoon.

Happy Birthday Meri Duniya, Meri Jaan, Mere Dil Ka Sukoon 💖

"Meri har dua mein tum ho, meri har khushi mein tum ho, aur meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat kahani bhi tum hi ho. Aaj ke din bas itna yaad rakhna, tum bahut special ho... mere liye sabse zyada."
