Even Silence Missed You

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj ka din thoda sa alag tha. Hum dono baat toh kar rahe the... lekin pata nahi kyun, dil me ek ajeeb sa distance feel ho raha tha.

Mai tumse naraz bhi tha... aur tumhe utna hi miss bhi kar raha tha.

Shayad pyar isi ka naam hai. Kabhi kabhi choti si baat bhi dil ko bohot zyada affect kar deti hai.

Aur jab insaan kisi ko sach me apna maan leta hai, tab uski khamoshi bhi bohot kuch keh jaati hai.

One Thought That Stayed

"I missed you... even while talking to you."

Shayad yehi feeling mujhe ajj sabse zyada yaad rahegi.

Har din perfect nahi hota... lekin har din tumhare saath hona, mere liye bohot zaroori hai.

See you tomorrow. 💜
