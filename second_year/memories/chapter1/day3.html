Your Little Caring Habits

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj wala din office me bohot busy tha, is wajah se hum zyada baat nahi kar paaye.

Lekin jab maine tumhe text kiya ki meri meeting hai aur mai baad me message karunga... tumne kaha,

"Chalega jao... lekin pehle mujhe ek snap bhejdo ❤️ Aur time se khana khana hai, nakhre nahi chahiye."

Pata nahi kyun... lekin tumhari ye choti choti caring baatein mujhe bohot achi lagti hai.

Tumne bola tha ki, "Ab wait nahi karungi... jao apni meeting hi karte rehna." 🫠

Lekin tumne exactly wahi kiya jo mai expect kar raha tha. 😭 Jaise hi mai wapas aaya aur tumhe message kiya... tumne turant reply kiya.

Aur haan... ek choti si complaint hai meri. 🙁❤️

Tumne bola tha ki tum mujhe Ammi ke haath se khana khate hue snap bhejogi... lekin tumne bheja hi nahi. 😭

Mai tumhe us waqt ye nahi bataunga. Mai wait karunga ki tum kab bhejogi vo snap.

Aur shayad isi liye mai ajj thoda naraz tha... Kyuki mere hints dene ke baad bhi tum samajh nahi paayi.

Kabhi kabhi... hum choti choti expectations bhi dil se laga lete hai na.

One Little Complaint ❤️

"I won't love you."

Obviously... mai jhoot bol raha tha. Tumhe kam pyaar karna shayad mere bas ki baat hi nahi hai.

Thank you for caring about me even in your own little ways.

Kal fir milte hai... ek nayi memory ke saath. 💜
