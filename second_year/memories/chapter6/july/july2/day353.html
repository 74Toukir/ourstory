A Little Heavy Heart

Assalamualaikum, meri Shifuuu. 💜🌙

Day 13 – A Little Heavy Heart 🤍

Good morning, meri jaan.

Aaj dil thoda bhaari sa hai...

Kabhi kabhi lagta hai zindagi har cheez ko thoda mushkil bana deti hai. Jis cheez ko dil se chahta hoon, us tak pahunchne ka raasta hamesha lamba aur imtihaan se bhara kyun hota hai... ye sawaal kabhi kabhi dil mein aata hai.

Kabhi main khud se poochta hoon... "Kya maine kabhi kisi ka bura chaha tha?" "Phir har baar itni bechaini aur itni fikr meri zindagi ka hissa kyun ban jaati hai?"

Sach kahun... Mujhe apni pareshaniyon se itna darr nahi lagta, jitna is baat se lagta hai ki kahin ye sab humare sapno ke beech na aa jaaye.

Lekin phir tum yaad aati ho. Aur tumhari yaad mujhe ek baar phir yakeen dilati hai ki Allah kabhi apne bande ko be-wajah imtihaan nahi dete. Shayad aaj ke mushkil din hi kal ki sabse khoobsurat kahani likh rahe hain.

Main bas itna chahta hoon... Ek din aaye jab main tumhe dekhkar kahun, "Alhamdulillah... ab sab theek hai." ❤️ Aur us din hum dono peeche mudkar in mushkil dino ko sirf ek yaad ki tarah dekhein.

Happy 13th Day of Birthday Month, meri princess. 💜

Aaj shayad main thoda thak gaya hoon... lekin tumse mohabbat aur humare future par mera yakeen aaj bhi utna hi mazboot hai. I love you endlessly. 🌙❤
