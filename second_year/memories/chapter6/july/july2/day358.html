Every Effort Is for Your Smile

Assalamualaikum, meri Shifuuu. 💜✨

Day 18 – Every Effort Is for Your Smile 🤍

Good morning, meri jaan.

Aaj Jumma ka mubarak din tha. 🤍

Allah ka shukr hai ki aaj bhi hume itna waqt mila ek dusre se baat karne ka. Humne itni saari baatein ki... aur har baat ke baad bas ek hi ehsaas hota raha ki tum meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat ne'mat ho.

Aur phir... tumne aaj jo snap bheji na... ❤️

Usse dekhte hi main kuch der ke liye bilkul kho sa gaya. Pata hi nahi chala kab main uss ek tasveer ko dekhte dekhte humare future ke baare mein sochne laga.

Main imagine kar raha tha... Ek din aisa bhi hoga jab tum isi tarah mere saamne hogi. Main tumhe dekh kar muskuraunga, tumse baatein karunga aur Allah ka shukr ada karunga ki unhone meri har dua qubool kar li. Ye soch kar hi dil ko kitna sukoon milta hai.

Aaj ka din thoda busy bhi tha. Office ka pressure bhi tha... aur saath hi ek aur zimmedari jo sirf mere dil ko pata hai. Tumhara birthday surprise. 💜

Sach kahun... Kabhi kabhi uski tension itni badh jaati hai ki darr lagta hai. Dil bas yehi sochta rehta hai... "Kahin itna bada surprise waqt se pehle complete na hua toh?"

Ye khayal mujhe pareshan zaroor karta hai. Lekin phir main himmat nahi chhodta. Har din uspar thoda aur kaam karta hoon, har chhoti detail ko aur behtar banata hoon... kyunki main chahta hoon ki jab tum ise dekho, tumhare chehre par jo muskurahat aaye na... woh meri zindagi ke sabse khoobsurat lamhon mein se ek ho.

Mujhe yakeen hai... InshaAllah main ise waqt se pehle complete kar lunga. Aur is baar tumhara birthday sirf ek din nahi hoga... Balki ek aisi yaad banega jise hum dono hamesha apne dil ke sabse kareeb rakhenge.

Happy 18th Day of Birthday Month, meri princess. 💜✨

Jumma ke is mubarak din meri ek hi dua hai... Allah humari har nek dua qubool farmaaye, humare raaste aasaan kare, aur ek din hume halal tareeke se hamesha ke liye ek saath likh de. Ameen. 🤲

I love you so much, meri Shifuuu.

Aur jo bhi mehnat main aaj kar raha hoon... uska har ek pal sirf tumhari ek muskurahat ke naam hai. 🌙❤️
