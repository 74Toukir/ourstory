Day 12 – My Favourite Part of Every Day Is You. 🤍✨

Good morning, meri jaan.

Aaj ek baat confess karni hai...

Pata hai, main kahin bhi jaun, kisi ke saath bhi hoon, ya kitna bhi busy kyun na hoon... Mere dil ka ek hissa hamesha tumhare paas hi rehta hai. ❤️

Agar bahar hoon toh sochta hoon, "Shifuuu ne khana khaya hoga ya nahi?" Agar phone vibrate kare, toh bina dekhe hi dil kehta hai, "Kaash tumhara message ho." Aur agar tum thodi der reply na karo... Toh chehre par smile hoti hai, lekin dil mein bas tumhari hi fikr chal rahi hoti hai.

Kabhi kabhi log sochte honge ki main phone itna baar baar kyun dekhta hoon... Unhe kaise bataun... Us screen ke us paar meri poori duniya baithi hai. 🥹💜

Mujhe duniya ki har jagah ghoomna pasand ho sakta hai... Lekin har jagah se zyada zaroori mere liye tumhare saath bitaye hue woh kuch minutes hote hain. Kyuki din kitna bhi achha kyun na ho... Agar tumse baat na ho, toh dil ko lagta hai jaise kuch adhura reh gaya.

Isliye agar kabhi tumhe lage ki main tumhe baar baar message karta hoon ya tumhari fikr zyada karta hoon… Toh uska sirf ek reason hai... Tum sirf meri mohabbat nahi ho... tum meri zimmedari, meri aadat aur meri sabse pyari priority ho. ❤️

Aur haan... Happy 12th Day of Birthday Month, meri princess. 💜🎂 Ab sirf 14 din baaki hain meri favourite date ke liye.

Chahe main kahin bhi rahun... meri nazar duniya ko dekh sakti hai, lekin mera dil hamesha sirf tumhe hi dhoondta hai. I love you endlessly. 💜🌙
