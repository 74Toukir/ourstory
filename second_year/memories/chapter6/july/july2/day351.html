In Every Crowd, I Will Still Choose You

Assalamualaikum, meri Shifuuu. 💜🌙

Day 11 – In Every Crowd, I Will Still Choose You. 💜

Good morning, meri jaan.

Aaj bas dil ki ek baat kehni hai...

Mujhe bas tum chahiye ho. Agar is duniya mein hazaaron log bhi khade ho na... tab bhi meri nazar sirf tumhe hi dhoondegi.

Kabhi kabhi mujhe pata chalta hai ki tum thoda insecure feel karti ho. Shayad overthink bhi karti ho. Aur sach kahun... mujhe bura lagta hai ki tumhe kabhi aisa mehsoos hota hai.

Isliye aaj ek baat dil se sun lo...

Chahe tum mere paas ho ya mujhse hazaaron kilometre door... mera dil hamesha tumhara hi tha, tumhara hi hai, aur InShaAllah hamesha tumhara hi rahega. ✨

Distance kabhi kisi ko replace nahi kar sakta. Time kabhi feelings ko kam nahi kar sakta. Aur koi bhi insaan meri zindagi mein tumhari jagah kabhi nahi le sakta. Tum meri choice nahi ho... Tum meri dua ho. Aur duaon ko replace nahi kiya jaata, unhe sambhal kar rakha jaata hai. 🥹💜

Isliye jab bhi kabhi tumhare dil mein ye khayal aaye ki "Pata nahi Toukir mujhe utna hi pyaar karta hoga ya nahi..." Toh bas aaj ka ye message dobara padh lena.

Har din badlega. Waqt badlega. Humari zindagi badlegi. Lekin ek cheez kabhi nahi badlegi... Mera tumse pyaar. ❤️

Allah humare dilon ko hamesha ek dusre ke liye mohabbat, yaqeen aur sukoon se bhar de. Ameen. 🤲

Aur haan... Birthday Month ka Day 11 bhi aa gaya. Ab sirf 15 din baaki hain meri princess ke special din ke liye. 🥹🎂

Happy 11th Day of Birthday Month, meri Shifuuu. 💜🌙

Mujhe is duniya mein sab kuch nahi chahiye... bas har dua ke baad, har safar ke baad, aur har kal ke baad... mujhe sirf tum chahiye ho. I love you endlessly. ✨

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Tum meri choice nahi ho... Tum meri dua ho. Aur duaon ko replace nahi kiya jaata, unhe sambhal kar rakha jaata hai."

Mujhe is duniya mein bas tum chahiye ho, meri jaan.

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
