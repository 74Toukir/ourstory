Day 19 – Even in Your Busy Day, You Remembered Me 🤍

Good morning, meri jaan.

Aaj hum zyada baatein nahi kar paaye... Aur iska reason bhi kitna pyaara tha.

Tumhare ghar mein mehmaan aa rahe the, isliye tum poore din unke saath busy thi. Mujhe pata tha ki tumhare paas bilkul bhi free time nahi hoga.

Lekin pata hai... Is sab ke beech bhi tumne mujhe yaad kiya. ❤️

Tumhare chhote chhote messages dekh kar dil sach mein bahut khush ho gaya. Un messages ne mujhe ye ehsaas dilaya ki chahe tum kitni bhi busy kyun na ho, tumhare dil mein mere liye hamesha ek chhoti si jagah rehti hai.

Aur mere liye ye baat duniya ki kisi bhi cheez se zyada keemti hai.

Aaj ki ek aur baat mere dil mein hamesha ke liye reh gayi... Jab tumne Arwa ko sulate hue jo snap bheji na... Main usse bas dekhte hi reh gaya. ❤️

Pata hi nahi chala kab us ek snap ko dekhte dekhte main humare future ke baare mein sochne laga.

Main imagine kar raha tha... Ek din InshaAllah humari bhi apni chhoti si duniya hogi. Tum isi tarah pyaar se hamare bachchon ka khayal rakh rahi hogi... aur main bas door se tum dono ko dekh kar Allah ka shukr ada kar raha honga.

Ye sirf ek khayal tha... Lekin us khayal ne mere dil ko itna sukoon diya ki main shayad lafzon mein kabhi bayan hi na kar paun. Kabhi kabhi tumhari ek chhoti si photo ya ek simple sa snap bhi mujhe itne khoobsurat sapne dikha deta hai ki lagta hai humara kal kitna haseen hoga.

Happy 19th Day of Birthday Month, meri princess. 💜✨

Aaj humari baatein kam zaroor hui... Lekin tumhari yaad bilkul bhi kam nahi hui.

Har guzarte pal ke saath bas ek hi ehsaas aur gehra hota ja raha hai... Main tumhe har roz pehle se thoda aur zyada mohabbat karne lagta hoon.

I miss you so much, meri Shifuuu.

May Allah protect you, keep you smiling, and one day turn all these beautiful imaginations into our beautiful reality.

I love you... endlessly. 🌙❤️
