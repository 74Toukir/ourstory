Aur isliye maine bina kisi sawaal ke maan liya. Kyuki jab meri Shifuuu mere liye kuch kehti hai, toh mujhe uski baat maan kar hi sukoon milta hai. ✨ 💜

Thank you meri jaan, mujhse pyar karne ke liye nahi... balki har roz itni care karne ke liye.

Happy 14th Day of Birthday Month, meri princess. 💜✨

I love you... aur tumhari har chhoti si care mere liye duniya ke sabse bade gift se bhi zyada keemti hai.
