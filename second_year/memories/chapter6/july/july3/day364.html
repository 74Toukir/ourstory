Every Tired Moment Is Worth Your Smile

Assalamualaikum, meri Shifuuu. 💜✨

Day 24 – Every Tired Moment Is Worth Your Smile 🤍

Good morning, meri jaan.

Aaj sach mein badan bhi thak gaya hai... aur aankhon mein bhi neend bahut hai.

Kal raat poori raat drive karke main wapas aaya. Safar ki thakaan abhi tak utari bhi nahi thi, lekin jaise hi ghar pahucha, ek pal ke liye bhi rukne ka waqt nahi mila.

Aaj Jumma ka mubarak din bhi tha. 🤍

Aur mere dil mein bas ek hi khayal chal raha tha... "Meri Shifuuu ka birthday surprise har haal mein waqt par us tak pahunchna chahiye."

Isi liye sabse pehle main tumhare surprise ka parcel courier karne gaya. Jab parcel finally courier ho gaya na... Tab dil ne ek gehri si saans li. Ek ajeeb sa sukoon mila.

Ye soch kar itni khushi hui ki... InshaAllah, meri Shifuuu tak mera ek surprise toh waqt par zaroor pahunch jayega. 💜

Lekin kahani abhi khatam nahi hui thi. Uske baad poora din laptop ke saamne hi guzra. Aaj lagbhag 18 ghante main sirf kaam karta raha.

Kabhi office ka kaam... Kabhi tumhare birthday surprise ka kaam... Aur har baar lagta raha ki abhi bhi bahut kuch baaki hai.

Kabhi kabhi dil thoda ghabra bhi jaata hai. Sochta hoon... "Kya main sab kuch waqt se pehle complete kar paunga?"

Lekin phir tumhari muskurahat yaad aa jaati hai. Aur us ek khayal se phir se himmat aa jaati hai.

Abhi meri back mein bahut dard ho raha hai... Isliye thodi der ke liye aaram karunga. Lekin ye sirf ek chhota sa break hai.

Kal raat 12 baje se pehle, InshaAllah, main is surprise ka bacha hua har hissa complete kar dunga. Kyunki main chahta hoon ki jab tum ise dekho... Toh tumhe sirf ek gift na dikhe. Tumhe meri har raat ki mehnat... Har din ki tension... Har dua... Aur har dhadkan usmein mehsoos ho.

Happy 24th Day of Birthday Month, meri princess. 💜✨

Aaj Allah se meri bas ek hi dua hai... Jis tarah tum meri zindagi ki sabse badi khushi ho, waise hi main bhi hamesha tumhari muskurahat ki wajah banu.

Aur agar is muskurahat ke liye mujhe kuch raaton ki neend, thodi si thakaan aur thoda sa dard bhi sehna pade... Toh mujhe woh bhi qubool hai. ❤️

I love you so much, meri Shifuuu.

Bas ek din aur... Phir tum dekhogi ki itne dino se main kis cheez ke liye itni mehnat kar raha tha. 🌙💜

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Tumhari muskurahat ke liye agar mujhe kuch raaton ki neend, thodi si thakaan aur thoda sa dard bhi sehna pade... toh mujhe woh bhi qubool hai."

Har mehnat, har dua aur har pal sirf tumhari ek muskurahat ke naam hai.

Counting down the final hours to bring that smile on your face...

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
