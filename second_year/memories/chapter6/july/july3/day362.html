Day 22 – Waking Up for Your Smile 🤍

Good morning, meri jaan.

Aaj ki raat bhi kitni khoobsurat thi.

Hum dono ne itni der tak baatein ki ki pata hi nahi chala waqt kab guzar gaya. Har baar ki tarah tumse baat karte karte dil ko itna sukoon milta hai ki lagta hai poora din kitna bhi mushkil kyun na ho, tumhari awaaz aur tumhari baatein sab thakaan door kar deti hain. ❤️

Lekin aaj mere dil mein ek aur cheez chal rahi thi...

Tumhare birthday surprise ka abhi bhi bahut saara kaam baaki hai. Aur jitna din paas aa raha hai, utni hi meri excitement aur thodi si tension bhi badhti ja rahi hai.

Tumhare sone ke baad main bhi so gaya... Lekin sirf sone ke liye nahi. Maine subah 4 baje ka alarm laga kar rakha tha.

Aur dekho... Alarm bajte hi main uth gaya. 🌙

Abhi jab sab so rahe hain, tab main chup-chaap baith kar sirf ek hi cheez par kaam kar raha hoon... Meri Shifuuu ke birthday surprise par. 💜

Kabhi aankhon mein neend hoti hai... Kabhi thodi si thakaan bhi mehsoos hoti hai... Lekin jaise hi main tumhare baare mein sochta hoon, saari thakaan kahin gayab ho jaati hai.

Bas dil mein ek hi khayal rehta hai... "Mujhe ye surprise har haal mein waqt se pehle complete karna hai."

Main chahta hoon ki jab tum ise dekho, toh tumhare chehre par jo muskurahat aaye... woh meri har raat ki mehnat aur har subah ki neend se kahin zyada keemti ho. Isliye aaj subah ki neend qurbaan karke bhi mujhe koi afsos nahi hai.

InshaAllah ye sab waqt par complete ho jayega. Aur phir main chain ki saans lekar sirf tumhari khushi dekhunga.

Happy 22nd Day of Birthday Month, meri princess. 💜✨

Abhi thodi der aur is surprise par kaam karunga... Phir subah thoda sa so jaunga taaki office bhi sambhal sakun.

Lekin ek baat hamesha yaad rakhna... Meri har mehnat, meri har dua aur meri har neend ki qurbani ka ek hi maqsad hai... Tumhari muskurahat. ❤️

I love you so much, meri Shifuuu.

Aur InshaAllah, bahut jald tum dekhogi ki main itne dino se kis cheez mein itna busy tha. 🌙💜
