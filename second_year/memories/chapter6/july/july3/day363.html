Thank You for Loving My Family Too

Assalamualaikum, meri Shifuuu. 💜✨

Day 23 – Thank You for Loving My Family Too 🤍

Good morning, meri jaan.

Aaj ka din meri planning se bilkul alag tha.

Itne dino se main tumhare birthday surprise ko lekar kitni mehnat kar raha tha. Har din bas ek hi soch rehti thi ki ise waqt se pehle complete karna hai.

Lekin kabhi kabhi zindagi achanak hume wahan le jaati hai jahan humne socha bhi nahi hota.

Aaj Nana ke inteqal ki khabar mili... Aur ye sunte hi mujhe turant gaon aana pada.

Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un. 🤲

Allah unki maghfirat farmaaye, unki qabr ko roshan kare aur unhe Jannatul Firdaus mein aala maqam ata farmaaye. Ameen.

Is sab ke beech ek aur baat ne dil ko aur bhi zyada pareshan kar diya... Ammi ki tabiyat bhi theek nahi thi. Unhe is haalat mein dekh kar dil bas Allah se unki sehat ki dua hi maang raha tha.

Sach kahun... Us waqt surprise, office aur baaki sab cheezein ek taraf ho gayi thi. Bas Ammi ki fikr aur ghar walon ki zimmedari hi dimaag mein chal rahi thi.

Jab maine tumhe bataya ki mujhe gaon jana pad raha hai aur Ammi ki tabiyat bhi theek nahi hai... Tab tumne bina ek pal soche mujhse sirf itna kaha...

"Tum Ammi ka dhyan do... unka khayal rakho." ❤️

Pata hai, us pal mujhe kis baat ki sabse zyada khushi hui? Ye dekh kar ki tum sirf mujhse hi mohabbat nahi karti... Tum meri family ki bhi utni hi fikr karti ho.

Aur meri nazar mein ye mohabbat ka sabse khoobsurat roop hai. Tumhari ye baat sunkar dil ko itna sukoon mila ki laga...

Allah ne mujhe kitni pyari aur samajhdaar humsafar likhi hai. 💜

Thank you, meri jaan... Sirf mera saath dene ke liye nahi... Balki meri family ko bhi apna samajhne ke liye. Ye ehsaas mere liye lafzon se kahin zyada keemti hai.

Happy 23rd Day of Birthday Month, meri princess. 💜✨

Aaj meri dua sirf itni si hai... Allah mere Nana ki maghfirat farmaaye, Ammi ko jald se jald shifa-e-kaamila ata farmaaye, aur tumhe hamesha apni hifazat mein rakhe.

Aur Allah hum dono ko hamesha ek dusre ke parivaar ki izzat, mohabbat aur khayal rakhne ki taufeeq ata farmaaye. Ameen. 🤲

I love you so much, meri Shifuuu.

Aur aaj tumhari ek baat ne mujhe ye aur bhi gehra ehsaas dilaya... Tum sirf meri mohabbat nahi ho... tum meri family ka bhi ek hissa ban chuki ho. ❤️

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
