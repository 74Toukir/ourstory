Day 25 – This Is Not the End, It's Our Forever Beginning 🤍

Good morning, meri jaan.

Aaj is diary ka aakhri din hai.

Ye likhte waqt dil mein ajeeb si feeling hai... Ek taraf khushi hai ki main is safar ko poora kar paaya, aur doosri taraf ye ehsaas bhi hai ki kaash main is diary ko kabhi khatam hi na karun.

Lekin phir dil muskurata hai... Kyuki ye sirf is diary ka aakhri panna hai, humari kahani ka nahi. ❤️

Sach kahun... Ye diary sirf kuch pages ya kuch likhe hue lafz nahi hain.

Iske har din mein meri ek dua hai... Har page mein meri ek yaad hai... Aur har line mein sirf tumhare liye meri mohabbat hai.

Aur mujhe yakeen hai... Ye sirf ek ending nahi, ye humari forever beginning hai. 💜

Aaj mujhe ek aur baat yaad aa rahi hai...

Do mahine pehle jab tumse galti se woh bottle toot gayi thi, tab maine tumse ek baat kahi thi. Maine kaha tha... "Mujhe pata hai, meri Shifuuu ise kabhi nahi phekegi." ❤️

Aur aaj... Poore do mahine baad tumne mujhe usi bottle ka snap bheja. Tumne kitni pyaari si muskurahat ke saath bataya ki... "Main isme plant grow karungi." 🌱❤️

Us pal meri aankhon mein sirf ek hi khushi thi. Isliye nahi ki bottle bach gayi... Balki isliye ki tumne meri di hui ek chhoti si cheez ko bhi itni mohabbat aur itni ahmiyat di.

Shifuuu... Tum meri har chhoti si baat ko yaad rakhti ho. Meri har chhoti si cheez ko sambhal kar rakhti ho. Aur ye ehsaas mere liye duniya ke sabse bade tohfe se bhi zyada keemti hai.

Thank you, meri jaan... Sirf us bottle ko sambhal kar rakhne ke liye nahi, balki meri har chhoti si yaad ko apne dil mein jagah dene ke liye.

Aaj ye diary khatam ho rahi hai... Lekin meri mohabbat kabhi khatam nahi hogi. Har naya din... Har nayi subah... Aur har nayi dua ke saath... Main tumse aur bhi zyada mohabbat karta rahunga.

Happy 25th Day of Birthday Month, meri princess. 💜✨

Agar kabhi saalon baad hum dono is diary ko dobara khol kar padhen... Toh main chahta hoon ki hum dono bas ek dusre ki taraf dekh kar muskura dein... Aur kahen... "Alhamdulillah... Allah ne humari har dua qubool kar li." 🤲❤️

I love you the most, meri Shifuuu.

Aur ye mera waada hai... Jaise aaj tum meri har chhoti si cheez ko sambhal kar rakhti ho... Waise hi main poori zindagi tumhari har khushi, har aansu, har sapne aur tumhara haath sambhal kar rakhunga.

This diary may end here... but my love for you never will.

Forever yours. 🌙💜
