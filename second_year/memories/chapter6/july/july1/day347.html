Home Is Wherever You Are

Assalamualaikum, meri Shifuuu. 💜🌙

Day 7 – Home Is Wherever You Are 🤍✨

Good morning, meri jaan.

Aaj subah ek baat dil mein baar baar aa rahi thi...

Log kehte hain "Home" ek jagah hoti hai. Lekin mujhe lagta hai... home koi jagah nahi hoti, ek insaan hota hai. ❤️

Aur meri duniya mein woh insaan sirf tum ho.

Jab tumse baat hoti hai, toh chahe din kitna bhi hectic ho, dil ko sukoon mil jaata hai. Tumhari awaaz, tumhari hasi, tumhara "Kya kar rahe ho?"... ye sab mere liye ghar jaisa ehsaas hai.

Kabhi kabhi sochta hoon... kitni ajeeb baat hai na? Hum dono ke beech itne kilometre hain, phir bhi jab tum saath hoti ho, mujhe kabhi akela mehsoos nahi hota.

Aur InShaAllah... ek din aisa bhi aayega jab "Call karte hain" ki jagah "Main aa raha hoon" kaha karunga. Jab phone ki screen nahi, balki tumhara chehra meri har subah ka pehla nazara hoga. 🤍

Us din shayad mujhe kahin aur sukoon dhoondhne ki zarurat hi nahi padegi... kyunki mera ghar meri baahon mein hoga.

Bas ek baat hamesha yaad rakhna...

Chahe duniya kitni bhi badal jaaye, meri favourite jagah hamesha wahi rahegi... jahan tum hogi. 💜

Aur haan... Birthday Month ka Day 7 bhi aa gaya. Ab sirf 19 din baaki hain meri princess ke special din ke liye. Har din ke saath meri excitement aur bhi badhti ja rahi hai. 🥹🎂

Happy 7th Day of Birthday Month, meri future wife. 💜🌙

I can't wait for the day when I won't have to miss you anymore... because you'll finally be home. ❤️

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Home koi jagah nahi hoti, ek insaan hota hai. Aur meri duniya mein woh insaan sirf tum ho."

Tumhare paas hona hi mere liye asli sukoon aur mera ghar hai.

I can't wait for the day when you'll finally be home with me.

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
