Birthday Month Day 2

Assalamualaikum, meri Shifuuu... 💜

Aaj ki baarish dekh kar bas ek hi baat dil mein aayi... Alhamdulillah. ✨

Allah ka jitna shukr ada karun utna kam hai ki unhone tumhe meri zindagi ka hissa banaya. Kabhi kabhi lagta hai meri har adhuri dua ka sabse khoobsurat jawab tum ho.

Aaj ka reminder sirf itna sa hai... duniya mein chahe kitne bhi log ho, meri favourite insaan sirf tum ho. Tumhari ek muskurahat meri poori duniya ko roshan kar deti hai, aur tumhari ek "kya karre ho and 👀?" meri thakan tak chura leti hai.

Aaj baarish ho rahi hai... aur pata hai? Baarish hamesha sukoon deti hai. Bilkul tumhari tarah. Tum saath ho toh har mushkil aasaan lagti hai, har pareshani chhoti lagti hai, aur har din thoda aur khoobsurat ho jaata hai.

Har guzarta hua din tumhare birthday ko kareeb la raha hai, aur meri excitement bhi utni hi badh rahi hai. Lekin birthday se bhi zyada, mujhe us din ka intezaar hai jab har baarish, har subah aur har shaam hum saath mehsoos karenge, InShaAllah.

Allah tumhe hamesha apni hifazat mein rakhe, tumhari har dua qubool kare, tumhare chehre ki muskurahat kabhi kam na ho, aur tumhe duniya aur aakhirat ki har khushi naseeb kare. Ameen.

Happy Second Day Of Birthday Month, meri princess. Thank you for existing. Thank you for making my life so beautiful. I love you endlessly. 💜🌙
