The Day You Changed My World 💜

Good morning, meri jaan.

Kabhi kabhi main sochta hoon... agar us din Allah ne humari mulaqat na likhi hoti, toh shayad meri zindagi aaj itni khoobsurat na hoti.

Sach kahun toh kisne socha tha ki Snapchat par shuru hui ek chhoti si conversation meri poori zindagi badal degi. Us waqt na hum mile the, na hi mujhe pata tha ki screen ke us paar baithi hui ladki ek din meri duniya ban jaayegi. Dheere dheere baatein dosti mein badli, dosti mohabbat mein, aur aaj meri har dua aur har future plan ka sabse khoobsurat hissa tum ho. Allah ki planning sach mein sabse behtareen hoti hai.

Tum meri partner hi nahi, meri sukoon ho, meri strength ho aur meri sabse badi blessing ho. 💜 ✨

Kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai Allah ne tumhe meri zindagi mein isliye bheja tha taaki mujhe life ka asli matlab samajh aaye. Tumne bina kuch maange mujhe itni khushiyan di hain ki main unka hisaab bhi nahi kar sakta.

Aaj bas itna kehna hai... thank you for walking into my life and changing it forever. ✨

Har guzarta hua din tumhare birthday ko aur kareeb la raha hai, aur meri excitement bhi badhti ja rahi hai. Dua karta hoon ki agle saal ka July hum aur bhi kareeb hokar celebrate karein, InShaAllah. ✨

Allah tumhe hamesha apni hifazat mein rakhe, tumhari har muskurahat hamesha salamat rahe aur humari story ko har buri nazar se mehfooz rakhe. Ameen.

Happy Birthday Month, meri princess. I love you more than yesterday and less than tomorrow. 💜🌙
