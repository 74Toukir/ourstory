Birthday Month Day 1

Assalamualaikum, Shifuuu 💜

Aaj July ka pehla din hai... aur mere liye sirf ek naya month nahi, balki saal ka sabse khoobsurat month shuru hua hai. ❤️

Happy Birthday Month, sweetheart ✨ 💜

Sabke liye July bas ek aur month hoga, lekin mere liye ye woh mahina hai jisne meri zindagi ko sabse khoobsurat banaya hai. Isi mahine meri princess is duniya mein aayi thi. Allah ka jitna shukr ada karun utna kam hai ki unhone mujhe tumse milwaya. ❤️

Humare saath ko ab 11 months hone wale hain. Long distance ne hume bahut kuch sikhaya hai. Sach kahun toh pichhle 11 mahino mein sirf woh din sabse zyada special the jab main tumhare saath tha—28 December, 6 April aur 17 May. Baaki saare din bas tumhari yaadon ke sahare guzre.

Kabhi kabhi lagta hai ki humare saath bitaye hue din ungliyon par ginne jitne hain, aur door rehne wale din itne zyada... Lekin InshaAllah ek din aisa bhi aayega jab iska ulta hoga. Jab tum meri wife banogi, tab hume saath rehne wale din ginne ka waqt hi nahi milega, kyunki har subah aur har raat ek dusre ke saath hogi. 🫠

Is poore birthday month mein meri bas ek hi dua hai... Allah tumhe hamesha khush rakhe, sehat de, har buri nazar se bachaye aur tumhari har dua qubool kare. Ameen. ✨

Aur ek baat... is July ka har din main tumhe special feel karwaunga. Har din tumhe yaad dilata rahunga ki tum meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat blessing ho. Sirf 26 July nahi, mere liye to poora July tumhara hai. ✨

Happy Birthday Month once again, meri princess. I love you so much. 💜🌙
