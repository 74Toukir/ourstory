Day 6 – Every Little Thing Reminds Me of You 🤍✨

Good morning, meri jaan.

Pata hai... aaj kal ek ajeeb si aadat ho gayi hai mujhe.

Din bhar kuch bhi dekhu ya sunu... sabse pehle tum yaad aati ho. 💜

Koi achha gaana sunta hoon toh sochta hoon, "Ye Shifuuu ko zaroor pasand aayega." Koi funny reel dikhti hai toh mann karta hai sabse pehle tumhe bheju. Baarish hoti hai, thandi hawa chalti hai, ya aasman mein chaand dikhta hai... har cheez mein bas tum nazar aati ho.

Shayad isi ko kehte hain kisi ko apni aadat bana lena.

Tum meri life ka sirf ek hissa nahi ho... tum woh wajah ban gayi ho jiski wajah se har chhoti si khushi aur bhi khoobsurat lagti hai.

Kabhi kabhi sochta hoon... kitni ajeeb baat hai na? Itni door hoke bhi tum meri har roz ki zindagi ka sabse important hissa ho. Aur InShaAllah ek din aisa bhi aayega jab mujhe tumhari yaad sirf phone ki screen par nahi, balki tumhare paas baith kar aayegi. 🤍

Bas itna yaad rakhna... chahe din kitna bhi busy ho, kitni bhi thakan ho, ya kitni bhi doori ho... meri favourite notification hamesha tumhara message hi rahega. 🥹💜

Aur haan... Birthday Month ka Day 6 bhi aa gaya. Ab sirf 20 din baaki hain meri princess ke special din ke liye. Main har guzarte din ke saath aur zyada excited ho raha hoon.

Allah humari mohabbat ko hamesha apni hifazat mein rakhe aur hume jaldi hamesha ke liye ek saath kar de. Ameen. 🤲

Happy 6th Day of Birthday Month, meri princess. 💜

I love you today, tomorrow, and in every lifetime Allah writes for us. 🌙✨
