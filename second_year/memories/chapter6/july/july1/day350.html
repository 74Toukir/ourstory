If Love Had a Name, It Would Be You

Assalamualaikum, meri Shifuuu. 💜🌙

Day 10 – If Love Had a Name, It Would Be You 🤍✨

Good morning, meri jaan.

Aaj Birthday Month ka Day 10 hai... aur aaj main sirf ek hi baat karna chahta hoon.

Tumse mohabbat. ❤️

Kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai ki "I love you" bahut chhota sa sentence hai... kyunki jo main tumhare liye mehsoos karta hoon, woh in teen lafzon se kahin zyada hai.

Tum meri aadat ho. Meri tasalli ho. Meri muskurahat ka reason ho. Aur meri har dua ka sabse khoobsurat hissa ho.

Pata hai mujhe sabse zyada kya pasand hai? Ye ehsaas ki duniya mein ek insaan hai jiske saath main apni har khushi, har pareshani aur apni poori zindagi baantna chahta hoon. Aur woh insaan sirf tum ho. 💜

Main perfect nahi hoon. Kabhi galtiyan bhi hongi, kabhi gussa bhi aayega, kabhi misunderstandings bhi hongi.

Lekin ek cheez ka wada aaj phir karta hoon...

Main har din tumhe choose karunga. Har mushkil mein. Har khushi mein. Har dua mein. Aur apni zindagi ke har naye safar mein. Meri mohabbat sirf aaj ke liye nahi hai… Main har nayi subah tumse thoda aur zyada mohabbat karna chahta hoon.

Aur InShaAllah, jab hum budhe ho jayenge na... Tab bhi main tumhe isi tarah dekhkar muskuraunga aur dil se kahunga, "Alhamdulillah... Allah ne mujhe meri sabse khoobsurat dua de di." 🤍

Aur haan… Birthday Month ka Day 10 Mubarak, meri princess. 🥹🎂 Ab sirf 16 din baaki hain us din ke liye jis din meri duniya ki sabse khoobsurat ladki is duniya mein aayi thi. 💜

I love you more than words can ever explain. More than yesterday, and less than every tomorrow Allah has written for us. 🌙❤️

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
