A Letter to My Future Wife

Assalamualaikum, meri future wife. 💜 🌙

A Letter to My Future Wife 💌

Meri pyaari Shifuuu,

Aaj ka message thoda alag hai.

Pata hai... aaj tum bina "Good Night" bole hi so gayi. Shayad kisi aur ke liye ye chhoti si baat ho, lekin mujhe thoda ajeeb sa laga. Tum aisa kabhi karti hi nahi ho.

Bas dil baar baar yehi pooch raha hai... tum theek toh ho na, meri jaan? 🥺

Umeed karta hoon ki tum sirf thak gayi hogi aur aaram kar rahi hogi. Agar aisa hai, toh please achhe se rest karna. Aur agar kisi baat ne tumhe pareshan kiya hai, toh yaad rakhna... tumhe sab kuch akele nahi sambhalna. Main hoon na. ✨

Kabhi kabhi main humare future ke baare mein sochta hoon. Sochta hoon ek din aisa bhi hoga jab mujhe ye sochkar pareshan nahi hona padega ki tumne good night bola ya nahi. Main khud tumhe blanket odhaunga, tumhara haath pakad kar dua padhunga aur kahunga, "Achhe se so jao, meri jaan. Main yahin hoon." InShaAllah.

Bas ek request hai... kal uthkar ek message zaroor kar dena. Sirf itna likh dena, "Main theek hoon." Mere liye wahi kaafi hoga.

Allah tumhe apni hifazat mein rakhe, tumhari har takleef door kare, tumhe sukoon bhari neend de aur hamesha khush rakhe. Ameen.

Aur haan... ❤️

Pata hi nahi chala dekhte dekhte Birthday Month ka Day 5 bhi aa gaya. Ab sirf 21 din baaki hain meri princess ke special din ke liye. 🥹🎂

Har guzarte din ke saath meri excitement aur badh rahi hai. Main bas dua karta hoon ki tumhara ye birthday tumhari zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat yaadon mein se ek bane. Aur InShaAllah, aane wale saalon mein tumhara har birthday hum ek saath mein celebrate karein.

Lekin usse pehle... meri sabse badi wish ye hai ki tum hamesha khush aur sehatmand raho. Tumhari muskurahat hi meri sabse badi khushi hai. 💜

Good Night, meri princess. I miss you... aur shayad aaj thoda zyada. Allah Hafiz. 💜

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

Tumhari muskurahat hi meri sabse badi khushi hai.

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
