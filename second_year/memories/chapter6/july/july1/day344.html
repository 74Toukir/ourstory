Day 4 – Your Smile Is My Sunrise 💜

Good morning, meri jaan.

Aaj raat se hi dil mein ek sukoon sa hai... aur uska reason tum ho. Shayad isliye kyunki aaj 2:14 AM tak tumse baat hui. Itni der tak tumhe dekhna aur tumhari khushbu ko mehsoos karna, mere liye kisi gift se kam nahi tha. 🤍

Pata hai, tumhari smile mein aisa jaadu hai ki meri saari thakan, tension aur pareshani kuch der ke liye khud hi gayab ho jaati hai. Log kehte hain subah ka suraj ek nayi umeed lekar aata hai... lekin meri har subah ki sabse khoobsurat umeed tumhari muskurahat hai. ❤️

Long distance humare beech zaroor hai, lekin meri har dua, har smile aur har heartbeat hamesha tumhare saath rehti hai. Aur jab bhi tum hasta ho, mujhe lagta hai sab kuch theek ho jayega.

Aaj bas ek chhoti si request hai... aise hi hamesha muskurati rehna, kyunki tumhari muskurahat sirf tumhare chehre ko nahi, meri poori duniya ko roshan kar deti hai. 🥹💜

Allah tumhe hamesha khush rakhe, tumhari har muskurahat salamat rakhe aur har din tumhari zindagi mein sukoon aur barkat bhar de. Ameen. 🤲

Happy 4th Day Of Birthday Month, meri princess. Bas 22 din aur... phir meri favourite date aa jayegi. I love you endlessly. 💜🌙
