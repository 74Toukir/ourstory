A Heavy Heart & Silent Apologies

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj dil bohot bhaari hai. Bas ek hi baat baar baar kehne ka mann kar raha hai...

"I'm sorry. Pata nahi tum mujhse naraz kyun ho gayi. Lekin agar meri kisi baat se tumhe bura laga ho... Toh dil se maafi chahta hoon."

Tum jaanti ho na... Jab tum naraz hoti ho... Mujhe sach me neend nahi aati. Aur ajj bhi shayad nahi aayegi.

Mujhe pata hai tum pain me ho. Aur shayad isi wajah se bhi tumhara mood theek nahi hai.

Lekin phir bhi... Dil bas yahi chahta hai ki tum jaldi theek ho jao aur phir se waise hi muskurao.

Ajj Good Night nahi bol paunga... Kyuki jab tak tum theek nahi ho jaati... Dil ko sukoon nahi milega.
