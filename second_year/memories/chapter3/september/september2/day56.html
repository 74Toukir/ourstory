One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Jaise door hokar bhi teri baahon mein so jaana." 🥹🤍

Kabhi kabhi bohot saare lafzon ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Yeh do lines mere dil ke us sukoon ko poori tarah bayaan kar deti hain jo mujhe har raat sirf tumhare baare me soch kar milta hai. Faasle chahe jitne hon, khayalon me tum hamesha mere paas hoti ho.

Thank you... Mere har din ka aakhri aur sabse haseen khayal banne ke liye aur door hokar bhi itna sukoon dene ke liye.

Khwabon me mulaqat hogi. Allah hafiz, Shifuuu. 🌙✨💜
