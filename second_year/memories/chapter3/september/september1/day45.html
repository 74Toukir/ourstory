Wool Shopping & Future Zid

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj tum wool lene gayi thi, aur wahan se tumne mujhe ek snap bheja.

Main uss snap ko dekhkar bas muskurata hi reh gaya. 🥹🤍

Pata nahi kyun, tumhe dekhkar ek hi khayal baar baar aa raha tha...

Ek din aisa bhi aayega jab tum bilkul bachchon ki tarah kisi cheez ke liye zid karogi aur bologi...

"Mujhe yeh chahiye." ❤️

Aur sach kahu...

Main chahta hoon ki uss din tumhari har chhoti-badi zid ko apne haathon se poora karun.

Tumhari khushi ke liye kuch laana, tumhari muskurahat dekhna, aur tumhari aankhon me woh excitement dekhna... shayad usse badi khushi mere liye aur kuch nahi hogi.

Pata nahi woh din kab aayega... Lekin uss din ka intezaar zaroor rahega. 🤍

Bas Allah se ek hi dua hai...

Ek din mujhe itna naseeb kare ki tum befikr hokar apni har zid mujhse kar sako, aur main har baar muskura kar keh sakun...

"Chalo, dilata hoon." 🥹❤️
