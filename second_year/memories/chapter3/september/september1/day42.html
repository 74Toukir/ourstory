Snaps, Bike Rides & "Humari" Zindagi

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj ka din snaps ke naam raha. 🤭💜

Pata hi nahi chala kab ek snap dusre snap me badal gaya, aur poora din bas hum dono ek dusre ke saath apne chhote chhote moments share karte rahe.

Aur madam ji ki demands toh din ba din badhti hi jaa rahi hain. 😌❤️

Ajj tumne kaha...

"Mujhe bike start karte waqt wala snap chahiye." 🤭

Ab itni saari lights aur buttons dekhkar bhi tumhara dhyaan sirf uss ek moment par tha. Aur phir tumne ek aur baat boli jo sunkar hasi bhi aayi aur dil bhi khush ho gaya...

"Ye tumhari bike nahi... humari bike hai." 🥹💜

Bas uss ek line ne dil jeet liya.

Pata nahi tumhe ehsaas hai ya nahi, lekin jab tum "meri" ki jagah "humari" bolti ho, toh har cheez aur bhi apni si lagne lagti hai. Jaise hum sirf cheezein nahi, apni zindagi bhi dheere dheere saath jeene lage hain. ❤️

Poora din hum isi tarah snaps bhejte rahe, haste rahe, aur ek dusre ki chhoti chhoti baaton me khushiyan dhoondte rahe.

Raat ko bhi humne kaafi der tak baat ki. Aisa lag hi nahi raha tha ki conversation kabhi khatam hogi. Lekin har din ki tarah, ajj bhi ek pyaari si Good Night ke saath hum dono ne apna din khatam kiya.

Ajj ki yaad bas itni si hai...

Ek bike, bohot saare snaps, aur "meri" se "humari" tak ka safar. ❤️

Good Night, Sweetheart. ✨💜

Allah humare is pyaare se rishte ko hamesha salamat rakhe aur humari har chhoti si memory ko zindagi bhar ki khushi bana de. Ameen. 🤲💕

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Ye tumhari bike nahi... humari bike hai." 🥹💜

Jab tum kisi cheez par apna haq is tarah jatati ho, toh mere liye uss cheez ki value hazaar guna badh jaati hai. "Meri" se "humari" tak ka yeh chota sa safar mere dil ke sabse kareeb rahega.

Thank you... Har choti cheez par apna pyaara sa haq jatane ke liye aur hamare kal ko aur bhi khoobsurat banane ke liye.

Khwabon me milte hain, Shifuuu. Allah hafiz. ✨🏍️💜
