Ammi's Payal & The Hair Band Snap

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj pata nahi kyun, subah se mujhe kal wala tumhara snap yaad aa raha tha.

Wahi snap jisme tumne apni Ammi ki payal pehni hui thi. 🥹❤️

Pata nahi uss snap me aisa kya tha... lekin usse dekhkar dil me ek alag hi sukoon mehsoos hua tha. Woh yaad ajj bhi poora din mere saath rahi.

Ajj jab main camp gaya tha, wahan maine Meensu ke liye ek hair band liya.

Jab maine tumhe bataya, toh tumne turant kaha...

"Ab woh pehenkar mujhe snap bhejo." 🤭

Aur phir... Main raste me hi woh hair band pehenkar tumhare liye snap lene laga.

Sach bataun, uss waqt aas-paas ke log mujhe dekh rahe the. Shayad unhe lag raha hoga ki yeh ladka achanak hair band pehenkar kya kar raha hai. 😂

Lekin mujhe kisi ki parwah hi nahi thi.

Tumne bola tha... aur mere liye bas itna hi kaafi tha.

Kabhi kabhi tumhari ek chhoti si baat ke liye duniya ki nazrein bhi ignore karna bohot aasaan lagta hai. Kyunki uske badle me jo tumhari smile milti hai... uski koi keemat nahi. ❤️

Ajj ki yaad bhi bohot pyaari rahegi...

Ek hair band, raste ka safar, aur tumhare liye bina soche kuch bhi kar dene ka ehsaas. 🥹🤍

Good Night, Sweetheart. 💜✨

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Ab woh pehenkar mujhe snap bhejo." 🤭❤️

Raste par khade hokar, logo ki parwah kiye bina tumhari khushi ke liye woh hair band pehenna mere liye ek pyaara sa moment ban gaya. Jab tum kuch kehti ho, toh poori duniya peeche chhut jaati hai aur bas tumhari smile yaad rehti hai.

Thank you... Mujhe yeh sikhane ke liye ki tumhari ek muskurahat ke saamne duniya bhar ki baatein kitni chhoti aur be-asar hain.

Apni is pyaari si zid ko hamesha barkarar rakhna. Allah hafiz, Shifuuu. 🎀✨💜
