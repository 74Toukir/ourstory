Poetic Longings & Pure Acceptance

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj hum bohot der raat tak baatein karte rahe. Maine tumhare liye kuch lines kahi...

"Tere saath ko tarse, teri baat ko tarse,
Yeh kaisa ishq raha humara meri jaan,
Tere hokar bhi teri ek mulaqat ko tarse."

Aur uske baad tumne jo kaha... Shayad woh meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat baaton me se ek rahegi. Tumne kaha...

"Sach me Toukir, aisa kuch bhi nahi hai. Tum jaise ho mujhe bohot pasand ho. Tum mujhe bolte ho ki tumhara gussa kharab hai aur tum naraz ho jaate ho, lekin yeh sab tumne mujhe kabhi nahi dikhaya. Mere saath tum hamesha apni soft side dikhate ho. Tum kitne bhi naraz raho, mere manane se maan jaate ho. Mere liye tum jaise ho waise hi bohot acche ho. Mujhe kuch bhi change nahi karna. I love you as you are." ❤️

Yeh padhkar meri aankhon me ek alag hi sukoon tha. Kabhi kabhi sirf ek insaan ka tumhe waise hi accept kar lena... Puri duniya jeet lene jaisa lagta hai.

Thank you... Mujhe waise hi pyaar karne ke liye.

Good Night, Jana. ✨💜
