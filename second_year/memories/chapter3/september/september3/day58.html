Hijab Snaps & The Dream of Leaving Together

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj tum didi ke saath bahar ja rahi thi.

Jaane se pehle tumne ready hokar, hijab pehenkar mujhe snap bheja. 🥹🤍

Pata nahi kyun, uss snap ko dekhte hi main kuch der ke liye khayalon me kho gaya. Main bas imagine karne laga...

"Ek din aisa bhi aayega jab tum isi tarah ready hokar mere saath bahar chalne ke liye aogi. Main ghar ke bahar tumhara intezaar karunga, tum muskuraate hue bahar aaogi, aur phir hum dono ek saath ghar se niklenge."

Kitna khoobsurat hoga na woh din... Na koi screen hogi, na koi distance... bas tum aur main.

InshaAllah, ek din yeh khayal bhi humari kahani ki ek khoobsurat memory banega.

Good Night, Jana. ✨💜
