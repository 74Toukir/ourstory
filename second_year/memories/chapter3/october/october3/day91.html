The Warmth of Morning Desires

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj subha maine tumhare liye ek Good Morning message likha tha:

"Good Morning, My Love. Every sunrise reminds me how lucky I am to have you in my life. Your love is the first thought that warms my heart every morning and the last that makes me smile before I sleep. I wish I could wake up next to you, hold you close, and whisper how much I love you. You are my heartbeat, my reason, my forever."

Jab maine yeh message bheja tha, meri sirf ek hi khwahish thi—ki tumhari subha meri wajah se thodi aur khoobsurat ho jaaye. Kabhi kabhi lagta hai ki agar meri ek chhoti si baat tumhare chehre par muskurahat laa de, toh mera poora din ban jaata hai.

Bas Allah se itni dua hai ki ek din mujhe yeh sab sirf message me nahi, balki tumhare saamne kehne ka mauka mile.

Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Tumhari subha meri wajah se thodi aur khoobsurat ho jaaye."

Subha-subha apne dil ki har baat tumhare naam kar dena aur yeh sochna ki tum ise padhkar muskuraogi, mere din ka sabse khoobsurat aagaz hota hai.

Thank you... Mere har din ka pehla aur aakhiri khayal banne ke liye, aur meri har subha ko apni mohabbat se roshan karne ke liye.

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
