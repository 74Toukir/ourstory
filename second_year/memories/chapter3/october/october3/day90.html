The Anchor of Constant Belonging

Anandupalan: Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj jab hafta khatam hone wala hai, maine mud kar dekha ki ye poora hafta kitni tezi se beet gaya. Zindagi ki is raftaar me sirf ek hi cheez hai jo badalti nahi—wo hai mera tumhare liye ehsaas. Chahe kaam kitna bhi ho, thakan kitni bhi ho, din ke aakhiri lamhe me jab mai ankhein band karta hoon, toh tumhara hi chehra zehan me hota hai.

Tum mere liye sirf ek insaan nahi ho, tum mera sukoon ho, mera ghar ho jahan laut kar mai duniya ke saare shor se door ho jata hoon. Yeh rishta meri rooh ka sabse zaroori hissa ban chuka hai.

"Is bhaagti hui duniya me, tum mera thehrao aur sabse haseen sukoon ho."

Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Tum mera ghar ho jahan laut kar mai sukoon pata hoon."

Duniya bhar ki bhag-daur aur pareshaniyo ke bich ye sukoon rehna ki koi mere liye dua kar raha hai, aur mera intezaar kar raha hai, mujhe har mushkil se ladne ki taqat deta hai.

Thank you... Mera constant belonging banne ke liye, door reh kar bhi mujhe apni chahat se bandhe rakhne ke liye aur har haal me mera sath nibhane ke liye.

InshaAllah hamara sath humesha mukammal rahega. Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
