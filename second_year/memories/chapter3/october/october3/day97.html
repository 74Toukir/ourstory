A Heart Willing to Set You Free

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Agar maine kabhi bhi tumhe kisi bhi baat se dukh pahunchaya ho... toh mujhe maaf kar dena. Pichle teen din se dil bohot bechain hai. Har waqt bas ek hi khayal aata rehta hai—Kya tumne meri proposal ke liye sirf majboori me haan kaha tha?

Yeh soch kar hi dil toot sa jaata hai. Agar sach me tum humari kahani ko yahin khatam karna chahti ho... toh main tumhe kabhi rokunga nahi. Mohabbat kabhi zabardasti nahi hoti. Agar tumhari khushi meri doori me hai... toh main usse bhi qubool kar lunga.

"Bas itna yaad rakhna... maine tumse sachcha pyaar kiya tha. Aur hamesha karta rahunga."

The Pain of Selfless Affection

"Mohabbat kabhi zabardasti nahi hoti."

Tumhari khushi ke liye khud ke jazbaaton ki qurbani dene ki baat sochna aur is gehre dard ko gale lagana, ajj ka sabse bhaari lamha ban gaya.

Mera har saans tumhare sukoon ki dua karega... Chahe tum sath raho ya na raho.

Allah hafiz... 🖤
