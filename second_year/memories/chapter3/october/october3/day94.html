The Magic of Everyday Routine & Sweets

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj humne kitni saari baatein ki. Ab toh tum roti banate waqt bhi mujhse baat karti rehti ho, pata hi nahi chalta waqt kaise nikal jaata hai. Aur ajj ek aur chhoti si memory humari diary me add ho gayi—ajj maine pehli baar tumhare liye chocolate order ki.

Ho sakta hai duniya ke liye yeh ek chhoti si baat ho, lekin mere liye yeh humari kahani ka ek aur khoobsurat hissa ban gaya. Mujhe accha lagta hai humari har chhoti si memory ko sambhal kar rakhna.

"Ek din jab hum yeh diary saath me padhenge... tab shayad hum dono in chhoti chhoti baaton par muskura rahe honge."

Ab mujhe bohot neend aa rahi hai. Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Pehli baar tumhare liye chocolate order ki."

Tumhare kitchen ke kaamon ke beech humari guftagu aur pehli baar tumhare liye ek pyara sa mitha tofa bhejna, ajj ke din ka sabse masoom aur pyaara pal ban gaya.

Thank you... Zindagi ke aam kaamon me bhi mujhe shaamil rakhne ke liye aur har chhoti baat ko khas banane ke liye.

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
