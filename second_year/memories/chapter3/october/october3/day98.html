If my absence brings you peace... then I pray you always stay at peace. I never wanted to become a reason for your pain. All the dreams we made together... the future we imagined... maybe they were never meant to become reality.

But I want you to know one thing. I tried. With all my heart... I really tried. If this is where our story ends... then I'll accept it. Not because I stopped loving you... but because I love you enough to let you choose your happiness.

Your absence will always hurt more than your presence ever did. But somehow... I'll learn to live with that silence. I'll carry every memory with gratitude. And every prayer I make will still include your name.

Goodbye, Shifuuu. May Allah always protect you, keep you happy, and give you every happiness you deserve. Bye. 🖤
