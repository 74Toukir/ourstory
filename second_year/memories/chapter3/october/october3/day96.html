The Unspoken Doubts & Hidden Tears

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj likhte waqt bhi aankhon se aansu ruk nahi rahe. Pata nahi kyun dil baar baar ek hi baat keh raha hai—Kya humari kahani yahin tak thi?

Ajj bohot baar himmat ki ki tumse kuch sawal puchun. Lekin har baar darr jeet gaya. Pata nahi kyun aisa lag raha hai jaise tum mujh par pehle jaisa trust nahi karti. Ho sakta hai main galat soch raha hoon. Ho sakta hai yeh sirf mera darr ho. Lekin ajj yeh saare sawal mere dil me hi reh gaye... aur shayad hamesha reh jayenge.

"Pata nahi humara kal kaisa hoga. Bas Allah se dua hai ki jo bhi ho... hum dono ke liye behtar ho."

The Fear of Losing Trust

"Har baar darr jeet gaya."

Aansoon bhari aankhon se diary likhna aur un ankahe sawalon ke bojh ko dil me dabaye rakhna ajj ka sabse dardnak manzar tha.

Khuda hamari kismat me sukoon likhe... Aur jo darr mere dil ko ghere hue hai, use door farmaye.

Allah hafiz... 🖤
