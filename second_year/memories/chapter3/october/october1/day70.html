Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj tumne sone se pehle mujhe apna snap bheja. Aur uske baad tum mujhe daantne lagi... Tum keh rahi thi...

"Dekho kitne bimaar lag rahe ho."
"Sara din ghoomna hota hai."
"Chutti wale din bhi tum ghar me nahi baithte."
"Tum mera sunte kyun nahi ho?"

Main bas tumhare messages padhkar muskurata raha. Pata nahi kyun... Mujhe bilkul aisa lag raha tha jaise tum mere saamne baithi ho aur mujhe pyaar se daant rahi ho.

Aur sach kahu... Mujhe uss daant me gussa bilkul nahi dikha. Mujhe sirf tumhari care nazar aayi. Har line ke peeche bas ek hi baat thi... Ki tum nahi chahti main aur bimaar padun.

"Kabhi kabhi kisi ki daant bhi kitni pyaari lagti hai... Jab uske peeche mohabbat chhupi ho."

Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Mujhe uss daant me gussa bilkul nahi dikha. Mujhe sirf tumhari care nazar aayi."

Jab tumne meri beparwahi par mujhe itne haq se daanta, toh dil ko ek ajeeb sa sukoon mila. Dur hokar bhi tumhara meri sehat par yun pareshan hona aur gusse ke bhes me apni chahat jatana aaj ke din ki sabse khoobsurat yaad ban gaya.

Thank you... Mujh par itna haq jatane ke liye, meri laparwahi par mujhe daantne ke liye, aur apne har lafz me mere liye fikr chhupakar rakhne ke liye.

Ab poora dhyan rakhunga apna. Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
