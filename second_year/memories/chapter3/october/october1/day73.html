The Most Beautiful Answer To My Prayers

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj dil ne fir se tumhare liye kuch likhne ko kaha. Pata nahi kyun... Jitna tumhare baare me sochta hoon, utne hi naye lafz mil jaate hain.

Ajj ki shayari sirf tumhare naam...

"Aankhon ki chamak, palkon ki shaan ho tum,
Chehre ki hansi, lamhon ki muskaan ho tum.
Dhadakta hai dil bas tumhari aarzu mein,
Phir kaise na kahun... meri jaan ho tum." ❤️

Kabhi kabhi lagta hai... Tum meri zindagi ka woh hissa ban chuki ho jiske bina har khushi adhoori lagti hai.

"Aur agar kisi ne mujhse poocha ki meri sabse khoobsurat dua kya hai... Toh mera jawab hamesha tum hi rahogi."

Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Mera jawab hamesha tum hi rahogi."

Tumhare liye dil me uthne wale jazbaat jab lafzon ki shakal ikhtiyar karte hain, toh rooh ko ek ajab sukoon milta hai. Tum meri zindagi ka woh anmol maqsad ban gayi ho jise khuda se maang kar maine apni duniya mukammal kar li hai.

Thank you... Mere shabdon ko haseen maani dene ke liye aur mere har din ko apni khoobsurat chahat se roshan karne ke liye.

Hamesha meri sabse keemti dua ban kar rehna. Allah hafiz, Shifuuu. 📜
