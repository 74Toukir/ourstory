A Heart's Longing & Promises Of Infinite Patience

Guidelines: Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj humne bohot saari baatein ki. Aur pata nahi kyun... Ajj dil ne pehli baar itni zor se tumse milne ki zid ki. Maine tumse kaha... "Mujhe tumse milna hai."

Tumne kitne pyaar se samjhaya... "Milenge hum... lekin abhi nahi. Thoda wait karo. Main ghar se aise bahar nahi aa sakti."

Aur sach kahu... Tumhari baat bilkul sahi thi. Ek pal ke liye dil udaas zaroor hua... Lekin ussi pal maine apne aap se ek promise bhi kar liya. Main tumhara intezaar karunga. Jitna bhi waqt lage. Kyunki mujhe tumse sirf milna hi nahi... Tumhari izzat aur tumhari comfort bhi utni hi zaroori hai.

"Mujhe tumse bina mile bhi utna hi pyaar hai... Jitna tumse milne ke baad hoga. Bas jab tum kaho... Usi din humari pehli mulaqat hogi."

Ab bohot raat ho gayi hai. Kal office bhi jaana hai. Aur mujhe pata hai agar tumhe pata chal gaya ki main itni der tak jaag raha tha... Toh madam fir se daantengi. Isiliye... Main bhi ab sota hoon.

Good Night, Sweetheart.
