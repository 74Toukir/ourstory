The Undying Longing For A Beautiful Mulaqat

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj dil me sirf ek hi ehsaas tha... Tumhari yaad. Aur uss yaad ko lafzon me likhne ki koshish ki toh bas yeh lines ban gayi...

"Tumhare saath ko tarse, tumhari baat ko tarse,
Yeh kaisa ishq raha humara meri jaan,
Tumhare hokar bhi tumhari ek mulaqat ko tarse." ❤️

Kabhi kabhi lagta hai ki hum roz baat karte hain... Roz ek dusre ki awaaz sunte hain... Roz ek dusre ki muskurahat dekhte hain... Lekin phir bhi dil ko sirf ek hi kami mehsoos hoti hai... Tumhari ek mulaqat ki.

Shayad mohabbat ki sabse khoobsurat aur sabse mushkil baat bhi yahi hoti hai... Insaan jitna kareeb hota hai... Dil utna hi aur kareeb aana chahta hai.

Bas Allah se ek hi dua hai... Jis mulaqat ka intezaar hum dono kar rahe hain... Woh din bhi jaldi naseeb ho.

Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Tumhare hokar bhi tumhari ek mulaqat ko tarse." 🥀

Rozana baaton aur digital mulakaaton ke bawajood jab dil sirf tumhare roo-ba-roo baithne ke liye bechain ho uthta hai, toh ehsaas hota hai ki yeh ishq kitna gehra hai. Ek mulaqat jo sabhi faaslon ko mita degi, usi ek pal ke khwaab me aaj ka din guzra.

Thank you... Mujhe is tarah apne khayalon me behlaane ke liye aur door rehkar bhi apni pakiza mohabbat ka ehsaas dilane ke liye.

InshaAllah humari mulaqat jald hogi. Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
