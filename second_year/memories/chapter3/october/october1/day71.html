Moonlit Dreams & A Heart Full Of Poetry

Guidelines: Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj fir se tumhare liye kuch likhne ka mann hua. Pata nahi kyun... Aajkal tumhare liye likhna meri aadat ban gayi hai. Jitna likhta hoon... Utna hi lagta hai ki dil me abhi aur bhi bohot kuch baaki hai.

Ajj ki lines...

"Chaandni raat ho aur tum mere paas ho,
Leherati hawaon me sirf tumhara ehsaas ho.
Tumhare kandhe pe sar rakh kar yunhi so jaun,
Aur khwabon me bhi bas tumhara hi saath ho."

Pata nahi kab woh raat aayegi... Lekin uske sapne dekhna bhi kitna sukoon deta hai.

"Bas Allah se dua hai... Ek din yeh sirf shayari na rahe... Humari haqeeqat ban jaaye."

Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Ek din yeh sirf shayari na rahe... Humari haqeeqat ban jaaye."

Tumhare liye jazbaat ko shayari me dhalna aur hamari ek haseen, sukoon bhari raat ka tasavvur karna dil ko behad pyara lagta hai. Yeh khwaab ki hum ek din sath honge aur tumhare kandhe par sar rakh kar zindagi bitani hai, meri zindagi ki sabse badi khwahish hai.

Thank you... Meri har ek lines ka zariya banne ke liye, mere khayalon me itna sukoon bharne ke liye, aur mere har ek sachay sapne ka hissa banne ke liye.

InshaAllah humari yeh khwahish zaroor poori hogi. Allah hafiz, Shifuuu. 📜
