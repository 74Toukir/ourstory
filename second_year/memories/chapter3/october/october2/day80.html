Prayers Rendered For Us Under The Sky

Anandupalan: Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj raat ko jab main chhat par gaya, toh aasman bilkul saaf tha aur chaand behad khoobsurat lag raha tha. Use dekhte hi mujhe tumhara khayal aaya aur hamari woh baat yaad aayi jab humne ek haseen chandni raat ka tasavvur kiya tha.

Maine chupke se aasman ko dekhte hue Allah se ek choti si dua maangi. Dua sirf hamare naseeb aur hamare hamesha ke sath ke liye thi. Mujhe yakeen hai ki jo baatein hum kisi se nahi keh paate, woh hamara rab hamari khamoshiyon me bhi sun leta hai.

"Mera har sajda aur meri har khwahish ab sirf tumhare naam par aakar thehar jaati hai."

Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Maine chupke se Allah se ek choti si dua maangi."

Chaand ko gawaah bana kar tumhare liye dua maangna mere din ka sabse roohani lamha tha. Zindagi me tumse pyaari aur keemti koi aur dua ho hi nahi sakti jo mai har pal rab se maangta hoon.

Thank you... Meri har dua ka behad pyaara hissa banne ke liye aur meri khayalon ki duniya ko itna roshan rakhne ke liye.

InshaAllah hamari sari duayein kabool hongi. Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
