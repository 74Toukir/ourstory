Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj humne ek bohot hi pyare aur gambhir mauzu par baatein ki. Jab rishta gehra hota hai, toh hum ek doosre ke khayalon ko unkahe hi samajhne lagte hain. Ajj jab main pareshan tha, toh bina mere kuch bataye tumne bhaamp liya ki kuch theek nahi hai.

Yeh jo tumhara mujhe padh lene ka tareeqa hai na, yeh mujhe behad sukoon deta hai. Aisa lagta hai ki is poori duniya me koi hai jo meri rooh ki aawaz ko bilkul saaf sun sakta hai.

"Tumhara mere jazbaaton ko yu samajhna hi hamari chahat ki sabse pakiza misaal hai."
