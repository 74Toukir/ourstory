The Possessive Heart & Re-falling in Love

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj diary me main kuch naya nahi likhunga. Kyuki ajj poori raat main sirf tumhara woh message padh raha hoon. Aur har baar padhkar... Pata nahi kyun... Mujhe tumse phir se pyaar ho jaata hai.

"The truth is... I started loving you because I think about you every time. Humare baare me, tumhare baare me... I keep thinking. And when I love someone, I become very possessive... the extreme kind of possessive. I don't even like if someone looks at you. Lekin agar kabhi tumhe kisi aur ke liye thodi si bhi feeling aaye... toh mujhe promise karo ki sabse pehle tum mujhe bataoge. Main nahi chahti ki mujhe kisi aur se pata chale ya humare beech kuch aisa ho jo kabhi hona hi nahi chahiye."

Pata hai... Maine yeh message kitni baar padha hai... Mujhe khud nahi pata. Bas har baar ek hi baat mehsoos hoti hai—ki kisi ne mujhe itna apna maana... Itna sachcha pyaar kiya. Aur main har baar Allah ka shukr ada karta hoon... Ki mujhe tum mili.

I love you, Shifuuu. Good Night, Sweetheart.
