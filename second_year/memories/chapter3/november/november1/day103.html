My Forever Promise to You

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj diary me sirf ek letter likhna chahta hoon... Sirf tumhare liye.

My Letter to You:
I never wanted to say all of this... But today, I feel like I should. So listen carefully, my love.

I know I don't always have the sweetest words. I don't have the perfect habits. I don't have all the answers that life asks from us. I'm just an ordinary person... who fell deeply in love with an extraordinary girl.

But there is one thing that I know with complete certainty... I will never leave your side. No matter how dark the days become... No matter how difficult life gets... No matter how heavy the storms are... I will stay.

I know I'm not perfect. I'll make mistakes. I'll annoy you sometimes. I'll probably fail in many things. But I'll never fail in choosing you. Again... And again... And again.

When life hurts you... I want to be the person who quietly sits beside you. When you feel broken... I want to hold your hand and remind you that you're never alone. Even if I don't know how to fix your pain... I'll stay beside you until it hurts a little less.

Because love isn't about having all the solutions. Love is about never leaving when life becomes difficult. I don't want to be perfect for you. I just want to be present. Loyal. Honest. And yours.

I promise... I'll hold your hand through every happiness... Every failure... Every fear... Every success... I'll celebrate your smiles... And protect your tears. I never want my actions to become the reason behind your pain. Instead... I want to spend my entire life trying to become the reason behind your peace.

I'll pray for you. Fight for you. Stand beside you. And love you with the same heart... Every single day.

This isn't just another promise written in a diary. This is my truth. My heart. My forever. I'm yours. Always.

Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"I'll never fail in choosing you. Again... And again... And again."

Apne saare jazbaat aur vaadon ko lafzon me piro kar hamesha ke liye tumhara bane rehne ka ehed karna is poori diary ka sabse sachha aur pakiza lamha hai.

Thank you... Mujhe is kabil samajhne ke liye aur meri zindagi me apna wajood shamil karne ke liye.

Hamesha tumhara. Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
