Hopes for Unhurried Moments

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj tum ATM ke kaam se bank gayi thi. Aur pata nahi kyun... Tumhari baat sunte hi mere dimag me ek ajeeb si umeed aa gayi. Maine socha... Kya yeh humare milne ka ek reason ban sakta hai? Kya tum kabhi kisi aise hi kaam ka bahana bana kar mujhse mil paogi?

Phir khud hi muskuraya. Bank ka kaam toh bas thodi der ka hota hai. Aur mujhe toh tumhare saath waqt chahiye. Sirf do minute ki mulaqat nahi. Main chahta hoon... Hum bina kisi jaldi ke baithkar baatein karein. Ek dusre ko bas dekhte rahein. Aur waqt ruk sa jaaye.

"Lekin abhi toh accident ke baad recover hone me bhi kitna time lagega... Pata nahi. Bas itna jaanta hoon... Jab bhi humari pehli mulaqat hogi... Uska intezaar zaroor kaabil-e-wasool hoga."

Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Uska intezaar zaroor kaabil-e-wasool hoga."

Mulaqat ke chhote chhote mokaon ki umeed lagana aur bina kisi jaldi ke tumhare paas baithne ka khwab dekhna ajj ke din ki sabse pyari soch hai.

Thank you... Aise har khayal se mere din me nayi umeed bharne ke liye aur mere sabse haseen intezaar ki wajah banne ke liye.

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
