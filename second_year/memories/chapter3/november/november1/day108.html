The Joy of Vanilla Ice Cream & Acceptance

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj tumne mujhe bataya ki tumhe vanilla ice cream kitni pasand hai. Aur phir humne tumhare liye order bhi kar di. Sach kahun... Tumhare liye kuch mangwana mujhe itna accha laga ki main bata bhi nahi sakta.

Pata hai mujhe sabse zyada kis baat ki khushi hui? Ki is baar tumne mana nahi kiya. Tum hamesha "Nahi... rehne do..." hi kehti rehti ho. Lekin mujhe na... Kabhi kabhi tumhare liye kuch karne do. Tumhare chehre ki ek chhoti si smile ke liye bhi main hazaar baar order kar dunga.

"Bas ek request hai... Aage se bhi kabhi kabhi meri baat maan liya karo. Mujhse kuch le liya karo. Tumhari khushi dekhkar jo sukoon milta hai... Woh kisi aur cheez me nahi milta."

Ab tum so chuki hogi. Aur main bhi ab sone ja raha hoon. Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Is baar tumne mana nahi kiya."

Tumhare liye vanilla ice cream order karna aur tumhari bina hichkichahat ki haan dekhkar milne wali bebaha khushi ajj ke din ki sabse meethi yaad ban gayi.

Thank you... Mujhe apne liye choti choti khushiyan bhejne ka haq dene ke liye aur meri parwah ko bina mana kiye qubool karne ke liye.

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
