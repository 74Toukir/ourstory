Pain, Healing & Longing

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj ka din shayad meri zindagi ke un dino me se ek tha... Jise main kabhi bhool nahi paunga. Ajj mera accident ho gaya. Sab kuch itni jaldi hua ki mujhe samajhne ka bhi waqt nahi mila. Poora din bas logon ke beech, hospital, medicines aur dressing me hi nikal gaya.

Ab raat ho chuki hai. Aur pata nahi kyun... Jaise jaise raat badh rahi hai... Waise waise dard bhi badhta ja raha hai. Har jagah dard ho raha hai. Lekin in sab ke beech... Sabse zyada ek hi cheez ki kami mehsoos ho rahi hai. Tumhari.

"Pata nahi kyun... Ajj sirf tumhare paas baithne ka mann kar raha hai. Bas itna sa ki tum ek baar keh do... 'Sab theek ho jayega.' Shayad uske baad aadha dard waise hi kam ho jaaye."

I miss you so much, Shifuuu. Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Sabse zyada ek hi cheez ki kami mehsoos ho rahi hai... Tumhari."

Accident ke itne shor aur dard ke beech bhi sirf tumhare shafqat bhare lafzon ki aas lagaye rakhna, mere dil me tumhari jagah ko zahir karta hai.

Thank you... Dard ke sabse mushkil lamhon me bhi mere dil ka sukoon banne ke liye aur meri har dua me rehne ke liye.

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
