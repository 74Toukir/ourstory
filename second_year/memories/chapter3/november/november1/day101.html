Shared Smiles & Future Streets

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj maine tumhe mera aur Shehzad ka bike wala snap bheja. Aur tum usse dekhkar kitna has rahi thi. Tum baar baar keh rahi thi... "Yeh itna dara hua kyun lag raha hai?" Sach me... Tumhari hasi dekhkar mera bhi din ban gaya.

Uske baad humne bohot saari baatein ki. Tumne bataya ki jahan tum rehti ho... Abhi tak tumhe waha ke raste bhi acche se samajh nahi aate. Maine socha... Aisa kaise hoga? Phir yaad aaya... Madam toh ghar se bahar hi nahi nikalti.

"Isiliye maine dil hi dil me ek aur sapna rakh liya. Ek din... Main tumhare saath wahi ki har gali me chalunga. Har raasta tumhe yaad karwaunga."

Aur uss din tum kahogi... "Ab mujhe sab raste yaad ho gaye." InshaAllah... Woh din bhi humari story ka hissa hoga.

Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Main tumhare saath wahi ki har gali me chalunga."

Bike wale snap par tumhara khilkhilakar hasna aur future me tumhare sath unhi raston par chalne ka sapna sajana ajj ke din ki sabse khoobsurat yaad hai.

Thank you... Meri har chhoti baat par muskuraane ke liye aur mere aane wale kal me apna rang bharne ke liye.

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
