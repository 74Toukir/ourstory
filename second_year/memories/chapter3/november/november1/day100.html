Tum Mile Toh Lamhe Tham Gaye

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj sara din bas ek hi gaana dil me chal raha tha. Aur pata nahi kyun... Har line me sirf tum hi nazar aa rahi thi.

"Tere siwa main jaun kahaan...
Koi bhi raah chunu, tujh pe hi aake ruku.
Tum mile toh lamhe tham gaye...
Tum mile toh saare gham gaye...
Tum mile toh muskurana aa gaya..."

Pehle bhi zindagi chal rahi thi... Lekin tumhare aane ke baad jeena accha lagne laga. Ab har khushi tumse judi hui lagti hai. Aur har dua me sirf tumhara naam hota hai.

Bas itna hi kehna tha ajj. Good Night, Shifuuu.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Tumhare aane ke baad jeena accha lagne laga."

Poore din dil me is gaane ki dhun gunjna aur har ek lafz me tumhari muskurahat ko mehsoos karna ajj ki sabse meethi yaad hai.

Thank you... Meri aam si zindagi me itne pyare rang bharne ke liye aur har dua ka hissa banne ke liye.

Hamesha meri muskurahat ban kar rehna. Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
