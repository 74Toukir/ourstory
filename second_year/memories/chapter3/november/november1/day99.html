Understanding and Unwavering Trust

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Pichle kuch din meri zindagi ke sabse mushkil dino me se the. Main bohot pareshan tha... Bohot saare sawal the mere dil me.

Lekin ajj maine tumhari har baat ko aaram se socha. Tumhare reasons ko samjha. Aur phir mujhe ek hi baat samajh aayi... Tum galat nahi thi. Sach kahun... Agar main tumhari jagah hota, toh shayad main bhi wahi karta jo tumne kiya.

Aur yeh samajhne ke baad mere dil ka sara bojh halka ho gaya. Bas ek baat hai jo main tumhe humesha kehna chahta hoon... Kabhi bhi yeh mat sochna ki main tum par trust nahi karta. Main tum par duniya se zyada trust karta hoon.

"Aur shayad isi trust ki wajah se... Main kabhi tumhara haath chhodna hi nahi chahta. Ab chahe kitni bhi mushkilein aaye... Main tumhare saath hi rahunga."

I love you, Shifuuu. Good Night, Sweetheart.

Hamesha ek doosre ka sahara bane rahenge. Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
