Prayers for Your Journey

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj se tum trip ki tayari kar rahi ho. Aur pata nahi kyun... Jitni excitement tumhe hogi... Usse kahin zyada tension mujhe ho rahi hai. Main bas yahi soch raha hoon... Tum itna lamba safar kaise karogi?

Aur upar se abhi tumhara fourth day bhi chal raha hai. Mujhe pata hai uss waqt tum kitni uncomfortable feel karti ho. Phir bhi tumne kitne pyaar se apne kapde, bag aur saari packing pehle hi decide kar li.

"Main bas tumhe dekhkar soch raha tha... Allah meri Shifuuu ka har safar aasaan kar de. Usse har takleef se mehfooz rakhe. Aur usse humesha apni hifazat me rakhe."

Ab tumhari journey ka intezaar hai... Aur meri dua bhi tumhare saath hi jayegi. Good Night, Jana.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Meri dua bhi tumhare saath hi jayegi."

Tumhare safar se pehle tumhari har chhoti takleef aur comfortable rehne ka khayal mere dil me basna, mere liye sabse khaas ehsaas hai.

Thank you... Aise muskura kar har safar ki tayari karne ke liye aur mere dil me duayein bhar dene ke liye.

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
