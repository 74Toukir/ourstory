Dreams of Mahabaleshwar

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj humne kitni der tak baatein ki. Tum mujhe trip ke baare me sab bata rahi thi. Aur phir tumne ek baat kahi... Ki waha tum mujhe miss kar rahi thi. Pata nahi kyun... Yeh sunte hi dil ko itna sukoon mila.

Phir hum dono ne ek aur chhota sa sapna dekh liya. Humne decide kiya... Ki ek din hum dono saath me Mahabaleshwar jayenge. Bas hum dono. Na koi jaldi hogi. Na koi tension. Sirf hum... Aur humari baatein.

"Main pehle se hij imagine kar raha hoon... Tumhara haath pakadkar waha chalna. Tumhara har jagah photo lena. Aur tumhara muskuraana dekhna. InshaAllah... Woh din bhi zaroor aayega."

Ab madam so chuki hain... Aur main bhi sone ja raha hoon. Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"InshaAllah... Woh din bhi zaroor aayega."

Mahabaleshwar ki sair, tumhara haath pakad kar chalna aur tumhari muskurahat capture karne ke sapne bunna, ajj ki raat ki sabse pyari khwahish hai.

Thank you... Trip me bhi mujhe miss karne ke liye aur mere sath ek khoobsurat kal ke sapne dekhne ke liye.

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
