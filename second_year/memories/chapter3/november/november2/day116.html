Planning Our Whole Life Together

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj raat humne phir se apne future ke baare me kitni der tak baatein ki. Pata hi nahi chala waqt kab nikal gaya. Hum soch rahe the... Humari zindagi kaisi hogi. Hum kahan rahenge. Kaise ek dusre ka khayal rakhenge.

Aur phir baat waha tak pahunch gayi... Jab tum apne Ammi Abbu se milne jaogi. Tab main tumhe lene kaise aaunga. Main kaise tumhara intezaar karunga. Aur phir tumhe wapas apne saath lekar aaunga.

"Yeh sab sirf baatein thi... Lekin pata nahi kyun... Mujhe lag raha tha jaise hum sach me apni future life plan kar rahe hain. Aur ussi future ko imagine karte karte... Kab hum dono ko neend aa gayi... Pata hi nahi chala. InshaAllah... Ek din yeh saari planning sirf planning nahi rahegi. Humari zindagi ban jayegi."

Good Night, Sweetheart.
