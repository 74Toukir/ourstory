Thoughts Across the Cold Distance

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj tum trip par nikal gayi. Lekin pata nahi kyun... Tumhare jaane ke baad mere dil ko bilkul sukoon nahi mil raha. Subah se bas tumhare baare me hi soch raha hoon. Maine tumhe safar ki dua bheji thi. Mujhe pata hai tumne woh zaroor padhi hogi.

Aur phir ek aur baat dimag me aayi... Itni thand hai... Aur tum sweater bhi itna patla sa lekar gayi ho. Mujhe toh pehle se hi pata hai... Tumhe thand bilkul bardasht nahi hoti. Abhi bhi lag raha hai... Pakka raste me tum keh rahi hogi... "Kitni thand lag rahi hai."

"Kaash main waha hota. Toh sabse pehle tumhe apni jacket pehna deta. Aur phir tum mujhe daantti... Ki tumhe bhi thand lagegi."

Bas Allah se dua hai... Tumhara safar bohot accha guzre. Aur tum khairiyat se wapas ghar aa jao. Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Kaash main waha hota..."

Tumhare thand me hone ke khayal se apni jacket dene aur tumhari pyari daant ko imagine karna, aaj ki sabse khoobsurat baat hai.

Thank you... Mere har khayal me bane rehne ke liye aur door hokar bhi mere dil ko apni fikr se mehfooz rakhne ke liye.

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
