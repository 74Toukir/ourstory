The Lit-Up Tree for Your Smile

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Pata hai... Main kitna pagal ho gaya hoon tumhare liye? Roz office jaate waqt ek lighting wala tree dikhta hai. Pehle bhi dikhta tha. Lekin kabhi uske paas rukne ka mann nahi hua.

Ajj pata nahi kyun... Mujhe yaad aaya ki tumhe lighting wali cheezein kitni pasand hain. Main wahin ruk gaya. Sirf isliye... Taaki uss tree ke saath tumhare liye ek snap bhej saku. Jab tumne woh snap dekha... Tum kitni khush ho gayi.

"Aur tumhari woh khushi dekhkar... Mujhe laga ki mera rukna bilkul sahi tha. Bas meri ek hij dua hai... Tum aise hij hamesha muskuraati raho. Kyuki tumhari smile hij meri sabse badi khushi hai. I love you, Shifuuu."

Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Tumhari smile hi meri sabse badi khushi hai."

Sirf tumhare ek baar muskurane ke liye raste me ruk kar snap lena aur tumhari khushi me apni khushi dhoondna, mera tumhare liye pagalpan bayan karta hai.

Thank you... Meri zindagi ke har chhote raaste me roshni ki wajah banne ke liye.

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
