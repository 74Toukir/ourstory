A Morning Made Beautiful

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj main thoda late utha. Jaise hi phone uthaya... Sabse pehle tumhara snap dekha. Aur usme likha tha... "Please wake up... I miss you."

"Bas... Ussi ek line ne meri poori subah bana di. Itni si baat thi... Lekin pata nahi kyun... Ek alag hi energy aa gayi. Aisa laga jaise poora din accha hi jaane wala hai."

Aur waise hi hua. Ajj humne bohot saari baatein ki. Hasna... Mazaak... Aur beech beech me woh pyaari si care... Ajj ka din bohot sukoon wala tha. Shayad isiliye kyunki meri subah hi tumhari yaad aur tumhare message se shuru hui thi.

Ab din bhi khatam ho gaya... Aur main bhi muskuraate hue sone ja raha hoon. Good Night, Sweetheart.
