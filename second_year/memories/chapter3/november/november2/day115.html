Sweet Mischief and Endless Patience

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj madam ne mujhe bohot sataya. Main kab se zid kar raha tha... "Snap bhejo..." Lekin tum bas mana hi karti rahi.

Phir tumne kaha... "Pehle bolo tum kaun ho?" Maine turant kaha... "Main Baby hoon." Phir bhi tum ruki nahi. Tumne phir pucha... "Jab hum milenge tab bhi bologe ki tum Baby ho?" Aur maine bina soche turant "Yes!" bol diya. Uske baad jaakar madam ne finally snap bheja.

"Pata nahi tum mujhe itna kyun satati ho... Lekin sach kahun... Tumhara satana bhi mujhe bohot pyaara lagta hai. Agar har snap ke liye itni si mehnat karni pade... Toh main har baar karunga."

Ab madam so gayi hain. Aur mujhe bhi sone ki permission mil gayi hai. Good Night, Sweetheart.

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Tumhara satana bhi mujhe bohot pyaara lagta hai."

Tumhare ek snap ke liye har shart manna, masoomiyat se 'Baby' kehna aur tumhari zidd ke aage bina shikayat ke haar jaana, aaj ka sabse pyaara ehsaas hai.

Thank you... Mujhe itna satane ke liye aur har satane ke baad ek pyaari si muskurahat dene ke liye.

Allah hafiz, Shifuuu.
