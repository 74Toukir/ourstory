"Hum kab aise saath baithenge? Kab main tumhara haath pakadkar bina kisi darr ke safar karunga? Kab woh din aayega... Jab tum mere paas baithi hogi... Aur mujhe sirf tumhara haath pakadna hoga."

Bas Allah se dua hai... Woh din bhi humari zindagi me zaroor aaye. Good Night, Sweetheart.
