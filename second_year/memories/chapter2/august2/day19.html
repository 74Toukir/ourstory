The Pink Shirt & Pyaari Chidhaan

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj subah meri aankh khuli toh sabse pehle maine tumhara message dekha ❤️

Tum Fazar me uth gayi thi aur maine uthte hi tumhe message kiya.

Uske baad mai nahaya, ready hua aur ajj maine green wali shirt pehen li 🙃

Fir chai pi aur office ke liye nikal gaya.

Lekin achanak mujhe yaad aaya...

Ajj toh tumne mujhe pink shirt pehenne ko bola tha 🫠❤️

Fir kya tha...

Mai wapas gaya, pink shirt nikali aur wahi pehen kar office aaya ✨❤️

Office pahunchte pahunchte tum bhi uth gayi thi aur fir humari baate shuru ho gayi.

Ajj office me zyada kaam bhi nahi tha, isliye humne aur bhi zyada baate ki.

Fir tumne mujhe snap bhejne ko kaha.

Aur jab maine tumhe bataya ki mai pehle green shirt pehen kar nikal gaya tha aur baad me mujhe tumhari baat yaad aayi...

Tum itna hasi 🤭❤️

Aur fir tumne mujhe "mand insaan" kaha 🤭❤️

Pata nahi kyun, lekin tumhara mujhe chidhana bhi mujhe bohot pasand hai.

Aur fir aise hi baate karte karte pura din nikal gaya.

Raat me bhi humne bohot der tak baat ki.

Aur ajj ek baat fir realise hui...

Koi bhi waqt accha lagne lagta hai jab vo tumhare sath guzarta hai ❤️

I love you, Jana 💜✨

Good Night ❤️
