Distance, Care & Gentle Comfort

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj wale din ki shuruwat toh hui... lekin tum ajj bhi pain me thi 🙁❤️

Aur sach kahu...

Ye mujhse dekha nahi ja raha tha.

Sara din tum thoda lazy feel kar rahi thi, aur maine tumhe bas ek hi baat kahi:

"Aaram karo, Jana." 🥺❤️

Mujhe pata hai jab tum pain me hoti ho toh tumhe chidchid bhi hone lagti hai.

Lekin ek baat yaad rakhna...

Tum kitna bhi gussa karo, kitni bhi chidchid karo, mujhe bura nahi lagega ❤️

Kyuki mujhe pata hai tum jaan bujhkar nahi kar rahi ho.

Tum bas dard me ho.

Aur jab tum dard me hoti ho, toh mera dil bas tumhare liye pareshan rehta hai.

Raat ko tumne kaha ki tumhe hot water bag chahiye.

Aur uss waqt pata nahi kyun, mujhe bas ye laga...

Kaash mai waha hota.

Mai tumhare liye paani garam karta, hot water bag laakar deta aur tumhe bas aaram karne bolta 🥺❤️

Thodi der baad tumne mujhe bataya ki Ammi tumhare liye hot water bag le aayi.

Aur ye sunkar mujhe thoda sukoon mila.

Maine socha...

Chalo, meri Shifuuu ko ab thoda aaram milega ❤️

Fir tum jaldi so gayi.

Aur mai bhi tumhare baare me sochte sochte raat ke 2:25 baje so gaya.

Good Night, Shifuuu ✨💜

Aaram se sona, Shifuuu. Khuda hafiz. ✨😚💜
