Morning Worries & Sweet Relief

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Mujhe yaad hai pehle tum mujhe batati thi ki jahan tum shift hui ho, waha abhi bhi tumhe sab kuch properly samajh nahi aata.

Isiliye ajj jab meri neend khuli aur maine tumhara message dekha:

"Mai doodh lene ja rahi hu."

Aur tum akeli gayi thi... 🙂❤️

Sach kahu toh mai thoda sa dar gaya tha 🙃🫠

Pata nahi kyun, lekin tumhari fikr hona ab meri aadat si ban gayi hai.

Fir maine tumhe Good Morning bola.

Thodi der baad tum online aayi aur tumne lift wala snap bheja jisme tumne bataya ki tum ghar wapas aa gayi ho ❤️

Aur uss moment mujhe sukoon mila.

Chalo... meri Madam Ji ko ghar ka rasta mil gaya 🤭❤️

Uske baad pata hi nahi chala kab din itna accha hone laga.

Kabhi kabhi din ki shuruwat me hi ek choti si baat pura mood accha kar deti hai.

Aur ajj mere liye vo baat tum thi ❤️

InshaAllah ajj ke din ka end bhi itna hi accha hoga jitni acchi iski shuruwat hui hai.

Chalo, ab mai uthta hu aur nahane jaata hu ❤️

Bye Shifuuu 💜✨😚
