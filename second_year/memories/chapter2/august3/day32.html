The First "I Love You" Voice Note

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj ka din humari kahani ki un yaadon me se ek ban gaya jo shayad kabhi bhool nahi paunga.

Main Bijapur se Osmanabad ja raha tha, aur tab tumne mujhe ek voice note bheja. Bas uske baad jo hua, woh mere liye bohot hi special tha. ❤️

Ajj humara pehla "I Love You" voice note tha. 🥹🤍

Maine tumhare liye apna "I love you" record karke bheja... aur jab tumne uska jawab diya, toh dil itna khush hua ki uss feeling ko shabdon me bayan karna mushkil hai.

Aur sabse funny baat pata hai? 🤭

Jab main voice note record kar raha tha, tab saamne ek aunty mujhe dekh rahi thi. Shayad unhe lag raha hoga ki yeh ladka itni muskurahat ke saath phone me kya bol raha hai. 😂

Lekin mujhe us waqt kisi ki parwah hi nahi thi. Maine toh ek baar nahi, do baar "I love you" record karke bheja. 🤭❤️

Pata nahi woh aunty mere baare me kya soch rahi hongi... lekin us pal mere liye sirf ek hi cheez important thi—tum. Aur tum tak mera pyaar pahunchna. ❤️

Ajj ka yeh chota sa voice note sirf ek audio nahi tha... yeh humari mohabbat ki ek nayi yaad ban gaya. Jab bhi kabhi ise yaad karunga, mere chehre par wahi muskurahat aa jayegi jo ajj thi.

Good Night, Baby. ❤️🌙
