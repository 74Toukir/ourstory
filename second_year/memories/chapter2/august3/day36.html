Jumma Blessings & Morning Priorities

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj main thoda late utha. Ajj Jumma tha, isiliye uthte hi sabse pehle tumhe message kiya. Uske baad nahaya, kyunki yaha sab log saath me rehte hain aur subah sabki apni apni taiyari hoti hai.

Lekin pata hai... uss sab ke beech bhi mere dil me bas ek hi khayal tha...

"Sabse pehle Shifuuu ko snap bhejna hai." ❤️

Jaise hi maine tumhe snap bheja, dil ko ek alag hi sukoon mila. Aisa laga jaise ajj ka mera sabse zaroori aur sabse pyaara kaam poora ho gaya ho. 🥹🤍

Shayad isi tarah dheere dheere tum meri aadat ban rahi ho... aur aadat se bhi badhkar meri priority. Ab din ki shuruaat tab tak poori si lagti hi nahi jab tak tumse baat na ho jaaye ya tumhe ek snap na bhej doon.

Mujhe yeh ehsaas bohot pasand hai... ki meri har subah me tum ho, aur meri har raat ki dua me bhi tum hi ho. ❤️

Bas aise hi meri zindagi ka hissa bani rehna. Kyunki ab meri khushiyon ki shuruaat bhi tumse hoti hai aur unka sukoon bhi tum par hi khatam hota hai.

Good Night, Sweetheart. ✨💜

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Ab din ki shuruaat tab tak poori si lagti hi nahi jab tak tumse baat na ho jaaye..."

Jumma ki subah ki vyastata ke beech bhi tumhara khayal sabse upar rehna yeh sabit karta hai ki tum sirf meri aadat nahi, balki meri zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat hissa ban chuki ho.

Thank you... Meri har subah ko ek pyari si wajah dene ke liye aur mere dil ka sukoon banne ke liye.

Hamesha aise hi pehli priority rehna, Shifuuu. Allah hafiz. ✨😚💜
