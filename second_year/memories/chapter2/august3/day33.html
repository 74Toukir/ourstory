Morning Demands & Good Mornings

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj subah jab meri aankh khuli, tab sabse pehla khayal tumhara hi aaya. Main Osmanabad me tha, lekin dil ki pehli aadat wahi thi... tumhe Good Morning bolna. ❤️

Jaise hi maine tumhe message kiya, madam ki ek nayi demand aa gayi... 😌

"Mujhe subah subah snap chahiye." 🤭

Toh phir humne bhi bina der kiye tumhe snap bhej diya. Aur uske baad tumne jo kaha, usne meri poori morning aur bhi khoobsurat bana di...

"Ab meri morning good ho gayi." 🥹💜

Aaj ka din waise hi bohot accha guzra. Humne baatein ki, hase, aur poore din bas ek halka sa sukoon sa raha. Kabhi kabhi kisi din ko special banane ke liye bade moments nahi chahiye hote... bas tumhara saath hi kaafi hota hai. 🤍

Bas ek choti si dua hai...

Har subah tumhari muskurahat aise hi bani rahe, aur agar ho sake, us muskurahat ki ek wajah hamesha main banu. 🫶❤️

Good Night, Sweetheart. 💜✨
