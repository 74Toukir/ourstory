Closer To You & Beautiful Imaginations

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj main Osmanabad se wapas aa raha tha.

Jab hum baat kar rahe the, tumne ek baat kahi jo mere dil me seedha utar gayi...

"Mujhe aisa lag raha hai jaise tum mere paas aa rahe ho." 🥹❤️

Pata nahi kyun, lekin tumhari yeh baat sunte hi meri soch kahin aur hi chali gayi.

Main imagine karne laga... ek din sach me aisa hoga. Main sirf khayalon me nahi, balki haqeeqat me tumhare paas aaunga. Tum saamne hogi, aur hum bina phone ke, bina kisi screen ke, ek dusre se baat karenge. Woh pehli mulaqat, woh pehli smile, aur tumhe dekhkar jo sukoon milega... bas ussi ke baare me sochta raha.

Aise hi tumhare saath apne future ke chhote chhote pal imagine karte karte pata hi nahi chala ki poora din kab guzar gaya.

Kabhi kabhi sirf ek sentence hi itne saare sapne dikha deta hai... aur ajj tumhari woh ek baat mere liye waise hi thi. ❤️

Bas Allah se ek hi dua hai... jis din ka hum sirf khayal karte hain, woh din ek na ek din zaroor aaye. Aur uss din hum dono bas ek dusre ko dekhkar muskura sakein. 🤍

Good Night, Sweetheart. 💜✨
