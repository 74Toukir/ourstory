Shared Responsibilities & Caring Thoughts

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Mujhe pata hai ajj tum bohot thak gayi thi.

Tumhare ghar mehmaan aaye the, aur mujhe pata hai ki aise waqt me zyada tar kaam tumhe hi sambhalna padta hai. Poora din tum busy rahi, isiliye hum zyada baat bhi nahi kar paaye.

Lekin pata hai...

Ajj bhi ek khayal dil me aaya.

Ek din humari kahani me bhi aisa waqt aayega jab ghar me mehmaan aayenge.

Aur uss din tum akeli sab kuch nahi karogi.

Main bhi tumhare saath khada rahunga. Kabhi chai banaunga, kabhi samaan uthaunga, kabhi kitchen me tumhari help karunga... taaki tum thak na jao. 🥹❤️

Mujhe nahi chahiye ki meri Shifuuu har kaam akeli kare.

Main chahta hoon ki hum har zimmedari bhi waise hi baant lein jaise hum apni khushiyan baantna chahte hain.

Ajj humne zyada baat nahi ki, aur koi baat nahi.

Kabhi kabhi pyaar ka matlab sirf baatein karna nahi hota...

Balki yeh samajhna bhi hota hai ki saamne wala thak gaya hai aur usse aaram ki zarurat hai.

Ab tum aaram se sojao.

Kal phir baat karenge, InshaAllah. ❤️

Good Night, Shifuuu. 💜✨

Thank you... Mere khayalon me ek pyaare se aane wale kal ka hissa banne ke liye.

Sukoon se sona, Shifuuu. Allah hafiz. ✨😚💜
