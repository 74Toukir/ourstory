Sweet Promises & Future Kheer

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj ki ek choti si baat ne mere dil ko bohot khush kar diya.

Tumne ajj kheer banayi thi aur phir pyaar se mujhse pucha, "Tumhe kheer pasand hai kya?" ❤️

Maine bina soche kaha, "Haan, bohot pasand hai." Aur uske baad tumne jo kaha, usne meri smile aur bhi badi kar di...

Tumne kaha, "Ek din mai tumhare liye bhi banaungi." 🥹🤍

Pata nahi kyun, lekin yeh ek simple si baat mere liye bohot special ban gayi. Shayad isliye kyunki us ek sentence me care thi, apnapan tha, aur ek choti si future memory chhupi hui thi.

Us pal maine bas imagine kiya... ek din sach me tumhare haath ki bani hui kheer khaunga. Shayad uska taste duniya ki kisi bhi kheer se zyada accha hoga, kyunki usme tumhari mehnat aur pyaar dono honge. ❤️

Kab woh din aayega mujhe nahi pata, lekin us din ka intezaar zaroor rahega.

Ajj ki yeh choti si baat meri yaadon me hamesha rahegi. Kabhi kabhi khushi badi cheezon me nahi, balki aise hi chote aur dil se nikle hue vaadon me milti hai. 🤍

Good Night, Shifuuu. ❤️🌙

Thank you... Mere har din me ek pyaari si umeed aur dher sara pyaar bharne ke liye.

Khush raho humesha, Shifuuu. Allah hafiz. ✨😚💜
