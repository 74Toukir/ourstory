Relief, Snaps & Sweet Understanding

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj ka din dil ko sukoon dene wala tha.

Sabse pehle, yeh dekh kar bohot accha laga ki tumhara pain pehle se kaafi kam ho gaya tha. ❤️ Alhamdulillah. Pichle kuch din se bas yahi dua thi ki tum jaldi theek ho jao.

Ajj humne achhe se baat bhi ki, aur tumne mujhe snap bhi bheja. Sach bolu toh tumhari ek choti si snap ne bhi mera pura mood itna accha kar diya. Tumhe better dekh kar dil ko bohot sukoon mila. 🥹❤️

Ek aur baat jo ajj mere dil ko touch kar gayi... tumne pichle 3 din ke behaviour ke liye mujhe sorry bola. Lekin Shifuuu, mujhe tumse kabhi us baat ka bura nahi laga. Mujhe pata tha tum pain aur stress se guzar rahi thi. Us waqt mujhe sirf itna chahiye tha ki tum theek ho jao.

Aur ajj jab tumne woh sorry bola, toh mujhe bas yeh feel hua ki tum meri feelings ki bhi itni care karti ho. Yeh baat mere liye bohot special hai. ❤️

Ajj ka din ek reminder tha ki mushkil din hamesha nahi rehte. Dheere dheere sab theek ho jata hai. Bas aise hi haste rehna, apna khayal rakhna, aur mujhe bhi apni smile ka reason banne dena. 🤍

Good Night, Shifuuu. ❤️🌙

Allah tumhe hamesha sehat, sukoon aur bohot saari khushiyan de. Ameen. 🤲💕
