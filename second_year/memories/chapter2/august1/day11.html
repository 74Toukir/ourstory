An Ordinary Day, Made Beautiful

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj ka din shayad un dino me se tha jisme kuch bohot bada ya special nahi hua.

Na koi lambi conversation hui, na koi surprise, na koi yaadgar moment.

Lekin pata hai?

Kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai ki pyar ki sabse khoobsurat baat ye nahi hoti ki hum har roz kuch extraordinary kare.

Balki ye hota hai ki ek normal sa din bhi kisi ki wajah se special lagne lagta hai.

Aur mere liye vo "kisi" tum ho. ❤️

Ajj sara din mai apne kaam me busy tha, lekin beech beech me tumhara khayal aata raha.

Aur har baar dil me ek hi feeling aayi...

"Alhamdulillah."

Alhamdulillah ki tum meri zindagi me ho.

Alhamdulillah ki mujhe tumhari fikr karne ka haq mila.

Aur Alhamdulillah ki har din ke end par mere paas tumhe yaad karne ki ek wajah hoti hai.

Agar kisi din humare paas likhne ke liye bohot sari memories na bhi ho...

Toh bhi mere paas tum hogi.

Aur shayad filhaal itna hi kaafi hai. ❤️

One Memory I'll Carry Forever

"Alhamdulillah... ki tum meri zindagi me ho." ❤️

Shayad ajj koi extraordinary memory nahi bani... Lekin kabhi kabhi ek simple "Alhamdulillah" hi dil ko sabse zyada sukoon de deta hai.

Thank you... Meri har ordinary day ko extraordinary feel karwane ke liye.

Good night, Shifuuu. ✨😚💜
