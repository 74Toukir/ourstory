A New Member In My Heart

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

I don't know mai har roz ye sab kyun likh raha hu.

Aur mujhe ye bhi nahi pata ki mai kab ye likhna band karunga.

Lekin jab bhi likhne baithta hu, aisa lagta hai jaise bas likhta hi jau... aur kuch na sochu.

Pehle lagta tha ki life me family hai aur unke liye bohot kuch karna hai.

Lekin ab lagta hai ki ussi family ka ek aur member badh gaya hai ❤️

Aur ab aisa lagta hai jaise uske liye bhi maine bohot kuch karna baki hai.

Ajj Jumma ki namaz se pehle tumne mujhse pucha tha...

"Agar mai tumhare kapde press karte waqt jala du toh tum kya karoge?"

Aur maine socha...

Agar tum mujhe kaho ki wahi jale hue kapde pehen kar bahar jao, toh mai bina kuch kahe pehen kar chala jaunga. ❤️

Isliye nahi ki kapde important nahi hai.

Balki isliye... kyunki vo tum ho.

Aur tumhari baaton ko maan lena mujhe kabhi mushkil nahi lagta.

Shayad ye hi batata hai ki mai tumse kitna pyar karta hu.
