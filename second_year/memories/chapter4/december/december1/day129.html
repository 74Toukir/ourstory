Your Mehndi & My Name

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj tumne mehndi lagayi thi. Aur sabse khoobsurat baat ye thi... Uss mehndi me tumne mera naam likha tha.

Subah jab tumne mujhe uska snap bheja... Sach kahun... Mera poora din hi ban gaya. Pata nahi kyun... Aisa lag raha tha jaise duniya ki saari acchi cheezein ek hi din me meri zindagi me aa gayi ho.

"Shayad isi liye kehte hain... Mohabbat mehngi gifts se nahi, Chhoti chhoti cheezon se aur bhi gehri ho jaati hai."

Ab tum toh so chuki hogi... Aur main bhi ajj bohot khush dil ke saath sone ja raha hoon. Good Night, Sweetheart.
