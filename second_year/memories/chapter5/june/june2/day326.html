Riverside Memories & Longing

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj main bahar gaya tha.

Highway ke side mein ek river hai.

Main wahan jaakar kuch der baith gaya.

Aur pata hai?

Wahan baithte hi mujhe tumhari bohot yaad aane lagi.

Main bas imagine kar raha tha...

Ek din hum dono bhi yahin aayenge.

River ke kinaare saath baithenge.

Bina kisi jaldi ke...

Bas baatein karenge.

Aur apna quality time spend karenge.

Pata nahi kyun...

Main har jagah humara future hi dekhne lagta hoon.

Aur har jagah tumhari kami mehsoos hoti hai.

I miss you, meri jaan.

Good Night, Sweetheart. 💜✨
