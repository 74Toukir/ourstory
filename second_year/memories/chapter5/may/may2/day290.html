Words Written For My Princess

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu, ❤️

Ajj main zyada kuch nahi likhunga.

Kyunki ajj ki sabse khoobsurat yaad woh message hai jo maine tumhe tumhe manane ke liye bheja tha.

Us message mein hum दोनों ki poori kahani thi.

Ek ladka jo apni princess se bohot zyada pyaar karta hai...

Aur ek princess jo us ladke se usse bhi thoda zyada pyaar karti hai. ❤️

Usme meri har galti bhi thi, meri har bewakoofi bhi...

Aur tumhara har baar mujhe maaf kar dena bhi.

Us message ko likhte waqt mujhe bas ek hi baat mehsoos ho rahi thi...

Ki chahe main kitni bhi galtiyan karu, main har din tumhare liye aur accha insaan banna chahta hoon.

Aur tum...

Tum meri wahi princess ho jise maine kitne saare naam diye hain...

Sweetheart. Destiny. Jana. My Love. Everything. Sunshine. Brightness. Cutie...

Aur pata hai?

In sab naamon se bhi zyada khoobsurat naam sirf ek hai...

Meri Shifuuu. ❤️

I love you so much.

Good Night, Sweetheart. 💜✨
