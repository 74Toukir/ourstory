Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Ajj mai ammi ko lekar jewellery shop gaya tha unke birthday gift ke liye. Tumhe pata hai waha ammi ne mujhe bola, "Ab tu apni wife ke liye bhi gold lena start kar de." Bas ussi waqt meri nazar waha ki jewellery par gayi aur mai sirf yehi imagine kar raha tha ki ye sab tumhare upar kitna khoobsurat lagega.
