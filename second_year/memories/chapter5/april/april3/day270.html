Always In My Heart

Asalamualaikum Shifuuu,

Pata nahi tumhe kya hua hai shifuu. Tum na mujhse baat kar rahi ho aur na hi mujhe bata rahi ho ki tumhe kya hua hai. Jana, aise nahi karna chahiye na. Mai bohot pareshan ho gaya hu. Bas itna bata do ki tum theek ho.

Mai itni dur hu, samajh hi nahi paa raha ki tumhare dil me kya chal raha hai. Kya ye humari uss mulaqat ki wajah se hai? Kya tum kisi baat ko lekar pareshan ho? Mai apne aap se itne sawal kar raha hu, lekin ek bhi jawab nahi mil raha. Bas Allah se yehi dua hai ki meri shifuu theek ho. I miss you so much. Ajj tumhe Good night kaise bolu... ye bhi samajh nahi aa raha.
